## Technical Notes

- Frontmatter in each blog uses: `image: public/blog-images/{filename}.png`
- BlogService normalizes this to: `/public/blog-images/{filename}.png`
- Until images are added, posts will show the fallback logo (/static/logo.png)
- No server restart needed — uvicorn --reload picks up new static files automatically
- Recommended image size: 1200×630px (OG/social) or 800×450px minimum for cards
