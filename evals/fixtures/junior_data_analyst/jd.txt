Data Analyst - Commercial Analytics
Location: Bengaluru (Hybrid)
Experience: 0-2 years

About the role
You will sit with the commercial team and turn raw transaction data into decisions the business actually makes. This is a hands-on analyst role with heavy stakeholder contact.

Responsibilities
Build and maintain data pipelines that move sales, inventory and customer data into our analytics warehouse.
Write efficient SQL to model and aggregate large transactional datasets.
Perform exploratory data analysis to identify trends, anomalies and revenue opportunities.
Design and publish dashboards in Power BI for category managers and regional leadership.
Partner with merchandising and marketing stakeholders to translate vague questions into measurable analyses.
Present findings to non-technical audiences and document methodology for reproducibility.

Required Skills and Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Engineering, Mathematics or a related field.
Proficiency in Python for data manipulation, including Pandas and NumPy.
Strong SQL skills, with experience querying relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
Hands-on experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
Familiarity with building ETL or data pipeline workflows.
Solid grounding in statistics and exploratory data analysis.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to work with cross-functional stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse.
Exposure to Apache Airflow for pipeline orchestration.
Familiarity with Git for version control.
Experience in retail, e-commerce or consumer goods analytics.
Prior work applying scikit-learn to a business problem such as churn or demand forecasting.
