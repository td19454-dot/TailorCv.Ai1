Senior Backend Engineer - Payments Platform
Location: Remote (EMEA/Africa)
Experience: 4+ years

About the role
Our payments platform moves money for 40,000 merchants. We are looking for a senior backend engineer to own core transaction services end to end, from schema design through production on-call.

Responsibilities
Design, build and operate high-throughput transaction APIs in Python.
Own service reliability, including on-call participation, incident response and postmortems.
Model and optimise relational schemas for correctness and performance under load.
Drive down latency and infrastructure cost across the payment path.
Mentor mid-level engineers and raise the engineering standard through code review.
Collaborate with product and compliance stakeholders on payment flows and audit requirements.

Required Skills and Qualifications
4+ years of backend engineering experience in production environments.
Strong Python, with deep experience in a modern web framework such as FastAPI or Django.
Advanced PostgreSQL: query optimisation, indexing strategy and partitioning.
Experience with Redis or a comparable caching layer.
Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production.
Experience running services on AWS.
Solid grasp of CI/CD practices and pipeline automation.
Demonstrated ownership of reliability outcomes such as latency, uptime or error budgets.

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Apache Kafka or another event-streaming platform.
Exposure to Terraform for infrastructure as code.
Prior work in fintech, payments or another regulated domain.
Experience with gRPC for internal service communication.
Familiarity with observability tooling such as Prometheus and Grafana.
