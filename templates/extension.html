FAQ

Have more questions?

What does the extension actually do?

On a job posting it reads the job description, then rewrites your saved resume for that exact role and downloads an ATS-ready PDF, without you leaving the page. The same panel will also write you a cover letter for that job, from the same resume.

Which sites does it work on?

Every major job board, plus company careers pages and ATS portals. The panel opens there by itself. On any other job page, click the TailorCV icon in your toolbar and it opens there too. If a page is unusual enough that we cannot read it, paste or highlight the job description yourself and tailor from that.

Do I have to upload my resume every time?

No. You save a base resume once and every job you open afterwards is tailored from it automatically.

Does it track where I applied?

Yes. Jobs you apply to through the extension are logged to your account, so you can see every application and the resume you sent.

Is it free?

Yes, the extension is free and a free account gets you started. Upgrade to Pro only when you want unlimited tailoring.
