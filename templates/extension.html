FAQ

Have more questions?

What does the extension actually do?

On a job posting it reads the job description, scores your saved resume against it, then rewrites your resume for that exact role and downloads an ATS-ready PDF, without you leaving the page.

Which sites does it work on?

The panel opens by itself on LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, Glassdoor and other big boards. On any other job page, a company careers site or a gig platform, click the TailorCV icon in your toolbar and the panel opens there too. If a page is unusual enough that we cannot read it, you can paste or highlight the job description yourself and tailor from that.

Do I have to upload my resume every time?

No. You save a base resume once and every job you open afterwards is tailored from it automatically.

Does it track where I applied?

Yes. Jobs you apply to through the extension are logged to your account, so you can see every application and the resume you sent.

Is it free?

Yes, the extension is free and a free account gets you started. Upgrade to Pro only when you want unlimited tailoring.
