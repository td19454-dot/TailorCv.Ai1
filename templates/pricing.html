Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ATS score really free?

Yes — forever. No paywall, no limit. It's our hook because we want everyone to benefit from it.

What does the "1 free" limit mean?

Each new account gets 1 free resume optimize, 1 cover letter, 1 mock interview, and 1 LinkedIn import. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited use.

Does the 7-day pass auto-renew?

No. It's a one-time purchase — pay once, get 7 days of Pro, done. No subscription, no charge after that.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

Same Pro features either way. Yearly billing saves you ~44% compared to monthly — you pay once a year at a lower effective monthly rate. You can cancel either plan any time; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Which payment methods are accepted?

UPI, debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, and wallets — all via Razorpay.

Can I still download my resume on the free plan?

Yes. Your 1 free optimized resume is fully downloadable in PDF — no watermark, no restrictions.

Is my payment information secure?

Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay — we never see or store your card details.
