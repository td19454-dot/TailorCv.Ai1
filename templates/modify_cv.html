FAQ

Have more questions?

What does the resume builder do?

It gives you clean, ATS-safe templates and guided sections so you can build a professional resume without wrestling with formatting, then download it as a polished PDF.

Are the resumes ATS-friendly?

Yes. Every template uses a single-column, parser-friendly layout so your resume is read correctly by the software and by recruiters.

Can I import my existing resume?

Yes, start from an existing resume and edit it in the builder, or begin from a blank template.

Can I tailor it to a specific job?

Yes. Once your resume is built you can tailor it to any job description and check your ATS match in a couple of clicks.

Is it free to start?

Yes. You can build and preview your resume for free. A quick free account saves your work and lets you download the PDF.
