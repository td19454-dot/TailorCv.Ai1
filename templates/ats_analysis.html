The changes most likely to raise your match rate for this job, in order.

Searchability

Important

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) parses your resume into fields that recruiters search. Below is how well your resume is read by an ATS and found in a recruiter search.

Tip: Fix the red Xs first. They are what stop your resume being parsed correctly or found by a recruiter.

Hard skills

High score impact

Hard skills are the job-specific tools, methods and technologies the role asks for. They carry the most weight in your match rate.

Tip: Match the exact spelling used in the job description, and only add a skill you can back up with a bullet in your experience or projects.

Soft skills

Medium score impact

Soft skills are how you work — communication, ownership, collaboration. Recruiters look for them in the way your bullets describe your work.

Tip: Show soft skills through results ("led a team of 4 to…") rather than listing them as adjectives.

Experience

High score impact

Your work history is the first thing a recruiter reads after the ATS passes you. These checks compare it with what this job asks for.

Tip: Start every bullet with a strong verb and end it with a result — a number, a time saved, a scale.

Projects

Medium score impact

Projects prove you can use the skills this job lists — especially when your work history is short.

Tip: Link each project to a repo or live demo, and say what it achieved, not just what it used.

Education

Medium score impact

Many ATS filters screen on a required degree or field of study before a person ever sees your resume.

Writing & tone

Recruiter tips

Once you pass the ATS, a recruiter reads your resume. Errors and filler are what make them stop reading.

Formatting

Important

Layout choices that look good to a person can scramble an ATS parser. These checks make sure every line of your resume is read.
