FAQ

Have more questions?

What is My Resumes?

It is your personal resume library. Every resume you tailor with TailorCV is saved there automatically, along with its ATS score and the job it was written for.

Do my resumes save automatically?

Yes. As soon as a resume is optimized it is stored in your library, so you never lose a version when you close the tab.

Can I re-download an old resume?

Yes, any saved version exports as a clean, ATS-ready PDF in one click, which is handy when a similar role comes up.

Can I see the ATS score of each version?

Yes, every saved resume shows its score, so you can tell at a glance which versions are strongest.

Is it free?

Yes. A free account saves your resumes and scores. Upgrade only when you want unlimited tailoring.
