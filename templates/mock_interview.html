What does the AI mock interview actually do?

It runs a realistic interview for your target role, asks real questions one at a time, listens to your spoken answers, and gives you a score with specific feedback on how to improve.

Are the questions specific to my job?

Yes. Pick a role or paste the job description and the questions match that exact role and seniority, behavioral, technical, and role-specific.

How is my answer scored?

You get a score plus judgment-free notes on structure, clarity, and content, so you know exactly what to say better next time.

Can I practice more than once?

Yes, run the interview as many times as you like and watch your scores improve with each attempt.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. You can run a mock interview for free. A quick free account saves your sessions and feedback so you can track progress.
