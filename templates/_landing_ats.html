FAQ

Have more questions?

What is an ATS score?

It is how well your resume matches a specific job in the eyes of the applicant tracking software that screens it. We score your resume against the exact job description you paste, then show you what is dragging the score down.

Is it really free with no signup?

Yes. You get one full ATS report with no account and no card. Create a free account and your ATS score stays free.

How is this different from the resume optimizer?

This checker audits your resume and tells you what is wrong. The resume optimizer goes further and rewrites your resume to fix it, then rescores it.

What does the report include?

Your match score and level, matched and missing hard and soft skills, a section-by-section breakdown, formatting and parser faults, and a priority list of fixes.

Is my resume stored?

Your resume is used to produce your report and is not posted anywhere. A free account lets you save your reports if you want them kept.
