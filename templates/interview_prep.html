FAQ

Have more questions?

What does the interview question generator do?

You add a role or job description and it generates the questions that role is most likely to ask, behavioral, technical, and role-specific, along with strong sample answers.

Are the questions specific to my job?

Yes. Paste the job description and the questions and answers are tailored to that exact role and seniority, not generic lists.

Do I get help answering them?

Yes, each question comes with a sample answer and the key points interviewers look for, so you know how to frame your own response.

How is this different from mock interviews?

This gives you a focused list of likely questions and answers to study. Mock Interviews let you practice answering them out loud with instant feedback, use both together.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. You can generate questions for free. A quick free account saves your prep lists so you can rehearse anytime.
