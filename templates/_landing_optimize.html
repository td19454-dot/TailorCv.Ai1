FAQ

Have more questions?

What does the resume optimizer actually do?

It compares your resume to a specific job description, scores the ATS match, surfaces missing keywords, and rewrites your bullets and summary to fit that exact role, then gives you a downloadable, ATS-ready resume.

Can I control what changes get applied?

Yes. You review every suggested edit and keep, tweak, or skip each one. Nothing is fabricated, changes are based on your real experience.

Does it work for all experience levels?

Yes, from first job and internships to senior and executive roles across every industry.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. You can scan and tailor your resume for free. A quick free account securely saves your resumes and scores.

What file formats can I use?

Upload a PDF resume; the optimized output downloads as a clean, ATS-friendly PDF ready to submit.
