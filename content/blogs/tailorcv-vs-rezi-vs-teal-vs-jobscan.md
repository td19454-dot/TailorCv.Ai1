## Jobscan: The Original Keyword Checker

Jobscan popularized the "match rate" score, but it only checks keywords - it doesn't rewrite your resume, doesn't do interview prep, and free scans are capped monthly. If you want the scoring without the scan limit, compare it directly on the [Jobscan alternative page](https://www.thetailorcv.com/jobscan-alternative) or read [best free ATS resume checkers compared](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/best-free-ats-resume-checkers-compared).

### Strengths of Jobscan:
- **Keyword Match Rate:** Jobscan focuses on providing a match rate score, helping you understand how well your resume aligns with a job description.
- **ATS Feedback:** It gives you insights into what keywords you’re missing, allowing for targeted improvements.

### Limitations of Jobscan:
- **No Resume Tailoring:** Jobscan does not assist in rewriting your resume, making it less useful for those who need comprehensive support.
- **Limited Free Scans:** The monthly limit on free scans can be a constraint for active job seekers.

### Best Practices with Jobscan:
1. **Use as a Supplement:** Consider using Jobscan in conjunction with a resume builder like TailorCV for a more comprehensive job application strategy.
2. **Focus on Keywords:** Pay attention to the keywords Jobscan highlights and incorporate them into your resume manually.
