## Best Content Writer Resume Format

1. Header with portfolio link (essential)
2. Summary
3. Skills
4. Work experience
5. Writing samples / published work highlights
6. Education

One page for most writers.

## Content Writer Resume Summary

Formula:

Content Writer with X years of experience creating [content types] for [industries]. Skilled in [SEO, copywriting, research]. Grew [traffic, engagement, or conversion metric] through content.

### Example for Experienced Content Writer

SEO Content Writer with 4 years of experience producing blog content, landing pages, and email copy for B2B SaaS and e-commerce brands. Wrote 200+ SEO articles that grew organic traffic from 12K to 58K monthly sessions. Skilled in keyword research, content strategy, and conversion copywriting. Proficient with Ahrefs, SurferSEO, and AI-assisted research workflows.

### Example for Entry-Level Content Writer

Content Writer with 1 year of freelance and internship experience writing blog posts, social media content, and product descriptions. Published 40+ articles across health, tech, and lifestyle niches. Strong in SEO basics, research, and adapting tone for different audiences. Seeking a content writer role in a marketing team.

## How to Write Content Writer Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Wrote / Grew / Created / Produced + [content type and volume] + [topic or audience] + [traffic, ranking, engagement, or conversion result]

### Weak Bullet Points

- Wrote blog articles for the company
- Created content for social media
- Did SEO writing
- Researched topics for articles

### Strong Bullet Points

- Wrote 120+ SEO-optimized blog articles in 12 months that grew organic search traffic from 14K to 52K monthly sessions, ranking on page 1 for 28 target keywords.
- Created a high-converting landing page copy that improved free trial signups by 31% after A/B testing against the previous version.
- Produced an email nurture sequence of 8 articles for new subscribers, achieving a 42% average open rate and contributing to a 19% trial-to-paid conversion lift.
- Researched and wrote a 4,000-word pillar guide that earned 12 backlinks from industry publications and became the site's top organic traffic page within 3 months.
- Built and managed an editorial calendar producing 16 articles per month across 3 brands, maintaining consistent brand voice and SEO standards while meeting all deadlines.

## Common Content Writer Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No results

Writing exists to achieve outcomes. Show traffic growth, rankings, engagement, or conversions. A writer resume with no metrics looks like a hobby.

### Mistake 2: No portfolio link

A content writer without writing samples cannot be evaluated. Always link your portfolio or published work.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring SEO

In 2026, most content writing roles require SEO knowledge. Show keyword research, on-page SEO, and ranking results.

### Mistake 4: Not addressing AI

Employers know AI writing tools exist. Position yourself as a writer who uses AI to enhance productivity while maintaining quality and originality — not one who fears or ignores it.
