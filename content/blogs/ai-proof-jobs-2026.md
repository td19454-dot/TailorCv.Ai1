Every industry is asking the same question in 2026: which jobs will AI replace, and which ones are safe? The honest answer is that almost no job is untouched - but some roles are far more resistant than others, because they depend on judgment, physical presence, trust, or accountability that AI cannot fully replicate.

## What Makes a Job Harder to Automate

- **High-stakes human judgment** - decisions with legal, medical, or safety consequences where accountability matters (a doctor, not just a diagnosis algorithm)
- **Physical presence and dexterity** - skilled trades, healthcare delivery, and hands-on repair work
- **Deep interpersonal trust** - therapy, sales relationships, negotiation, leadership
- **Ambiguous, evolving problems** - strategy, creative direction, and complex stakeholder management where the "requirements" keep changing
- **Regulatory or ethical oversight** - roles that exist specifically to supervise systems, including AI itself

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will AI eventually replace every job?

Unlikely in the near term - most roles are being reshaped by AI rather than eliminated outright. The bigger near-term risk is falling behind peers who use AI tools effectively within their field.

### Should I avoid tech jobs because of AI?

No - many tech roles, especially those focused on building, evaluating, or governing AI systems, are among the fastest-growing. See [resume for AI engineer](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-for-ai-engineer-2026) and [prompt engineer resume 2026](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/prompt-engineer-resume-2026).

### How do I show AI resistance on my resume?
