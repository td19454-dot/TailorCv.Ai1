## Why ATS Matters Even More for Freshers

Entry-level roles attract huge numbers of applicants, so employers rely heavily on ATS filtering. For freshers, that means your resume has to be keyword-relevant and clean — because you cannot lean on years of experience to carry it.

The score still measures **match**, not seniority. A well-tailored fresher resume can outrank an experienced candidate's generic one.

---

## What Freshers Should Emphasize

Since you have limited work history, your ATS score depends on highlighting:

| Section | Why It Helps Your Score |
|---|---|
| Projects | Demonstrates applied skills and keywords |
| Internships | Real-world experience the ATS recognizes |
| Skills | Direct match to job requirements |
| Certifications | Validates abilities, adds keywords |
| Education | Degree, coursework, achievements |

For project tips, read [how to add projects in your resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/projects-in-resume) and [technical skills for freshers with no experience](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/technical-skills-in-resume-for-freshers-with-no-experience).

## Step-by-Step: Raise Your Fresher ATS Score

### 1. Start With an ATS-Friendly Template

Use a clean, single-column [ATS-friendly template](https://www.thetailorcv.com/templates). Avoid the flashy designs that break parsing.

### 2. Mirror the Job Description

List the skills and tools in the posting and include the ones you have — from coursework, projects, or internships. See the [resume keywords guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-keywords-guide).

### 3. Build a Strong Projects Section

Treat academic and personal projects like experience:

- Project name and your role
- Tools and technologies used (keywords!)
- A measurable outcome or result

Example: "Built a web app using React and Firebase; reduced page load time by 40%."

### 4. Add a Skills Section

Include technical skills, tools, and a few relevant [soft skills](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/soft-skills-for-resume) that appear in the posting.

### 5. List Certifications

Free and low-cost certifications add keywords and credibility. See [best free online certificates for your resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/best-free-online-certificates-for-resume).

### 6. Write a Targeted Summary

A short summary naming the role and your top skills boosts keyword relevance. See [how to write a resume summary with no experience](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-summary-with-no-experience).

### 7. Scan and Refine

Run the [ATS score checker](/solutions), apply the suggestions, and re-scan until you hit 80+.

## Fresher Resume Mistakes That Lower ATS Scores

- Using a colorful two-column template
- Listing only responsibilities, no results
- Skipping a skills section
- Sending the same resume to every job
- Burying contact info in the header

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good ATS score for a fresher?

The same as everyone else — aim for 80 or higher. Your projects, skills, and keywords get you there. See [what is a good ATS score](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-a-good-ats-score).

### Can I pass ATS with no work experience?

Yes. Emphasize projects, internships, certifications, and skills that match the job. The ATS measures relevance, not just years.

### Should freshers use a one-page resume?

Yes. One clean, single-column page is ideal for freshers and parses well in ATS.

### How do I add keywords without lying?

Pull keywords from your real coursework, projects, certifications, and internships. Use the exact terms from the posting where they genuinely apply.
