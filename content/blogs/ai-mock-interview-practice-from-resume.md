## Why Resume-Based Practice Works

Interviewers usually ask about:

- Projects listed on your resume
- Tools you claim to know
- Impact in your bullets
- Career decisions
- Challenges and tradeoffs

If your practice questions are generic, you may still freeze when asked about your own work.

## What AI Can Ask

An AI mock interview can generate:

- Technical questions from your projects
- Behavioral questions from your experience
- Follow-up questions based on your answers
- Role-specific questions
- Feedback on clarity and structure

This helps you practice both content and delivery.

## How to Prepare

Before starting, review your resume and mark:

- 3 strongest achievements
- 2 difficult projects
- 1 failure or challenge
- 1 leadership example
- 1 teamwork example

These become your story bank.

## Use STAR for Behavioral Answers

STAR means:

- Situation
- Task
- Action
- Result

Example:

> Situation: Our dashboard was missing weekly revenue updates.  
> Task: I had to automate reporting.  
> Action: I built a SQL and Power BI workflow.  
> Result: Reporting time dropped from 5 hours to 45 minutes.

## Practice Project Explanations

For each project, prepare:

- What problem you solved
- Why it mattered
- What you built
- What tools you used
- What tradeoffs you made
- What you would improve

## FAQ
### Is AI mock interview practice enough?

It is excellent practice, but also speak answers out loud and record yourself.

### Can AI help technical interviews?

Yes, especially for project discussion and system design basics. For coding rounds, combine it with coding practice.

### Should I practice before every interview?

Yes. Even 20 minutes of role-specific practice can improve clarity.
