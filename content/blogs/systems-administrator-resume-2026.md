## Key Takeaways

- Sysadmin hiring managers look for uptime, scale (servers, users, sites) and automation - scripts that removed manual work.
- Split your skills by platform: Windows Server, Linux, virtualisation, cloud, scripting. Ads usually lean toward one side, and your resume should lean with them.
- Automation is the biggest differentiator in 2026. A PowerShell, Bash or Ansible bullet with time saved beats five "maintained servers" bullets.
- Mirror the job ad's environment: an "Azure and Intune" ad and a "RHEL and VMware" ad want different top bullets from the same resume.

A systems administrator resume earns callbacks when it shows that the systems you run stay up, scale well and need less manual work every quarter. Numbers make that visible: how many servers or users, what uptime, how many hours your automation saved.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Maintain Windows and Linux server environments" | State the count and mix: "120 servers (80 Windows Server 2019/2022, 40 RHEL 8)". |
| "Automate routine tasks" | At least one scripting bullet with hours saved. |
| "Ensure system availability and security patching" | An uptime figure and a patching cadence or compliance rate. |
| "Experience with cloud (Azure/AWS)" | Name the services you managed, not just "cloud". |

**Before:** Maintained company servers.

## Tailoring Walkthrough

- **Windows/Microsoft-heavy ad:** lead with Active Directory, Group Policy, Intune and Microsoft 365 bullets.

## Common Mistakes

1. **"Responsible for" bullets.** They describe duties, not results.
2. **No scale.** "Managed servers" could mean 3 or 3,000.
3. **Ignoring security.** Patching, hardening and access reviews are core sysadmin work in 2026 - show them.
4. **Burying automation.** Put your best scripting bullet in the top three.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a sysadmin and a DevOps resume?
A sysadmin resume centres on running and securing systems - uptime, patching, identity, backups. A DevOps resume centres on delivery - CI/CD pipelines, containers and infrastructure-as-code. If you do both, lead with whichever the job ad emphasises.

### Which certifications help a systems administrator resume?
Microsoft (Azure Administrator AZ-104), Red Hat (RHCSA/RHCE), CompTIA Linux+ and Server+, and VMware VCP are the most recognised. List only the ones you hold.

### How do I show automation if my scripts were small?
Small scripts still count when you show their effect: "saved 15 minutes per new hire" or "removed a 2-hour manual report each week". The time saved matters more than the script's size.
