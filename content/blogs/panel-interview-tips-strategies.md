## What Is a Panel Interview?

A panel [interview](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-a-job-interview-in-2026-complete-guide-for-every-industry) is a single [interview](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-a-job-interview-in-2026-complete-guide-for-every-industry) session with multiple interviewers - often a hiring manager, team member, HR representative, and sometimes a senior leader. Common in:

- Enterprise and government roles
- Academic and healthcare positions
- Final-round interviews at tech companies
- [Final round interview stages](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/final-round-interview-tips)

Related formats: [group interviews](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/group-interview-tips-strategies) (multiple candidates) vs. panel (multiple interviewers, one candidate).

## Before the Panel Interview

### Research every interviewer (when possible)

[LinkedIn](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-use-linkedin-for-job-search) is your friend. Know each person's role so you can tailor examples:

- **Engineering manager** - impact, ownership, technical depth
- **HR partner** - culture fit, [behavioral questions](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/behavioral-interview-questions-and-answers)
- **Peer engineer** - collaboration, day-to-day skills

### Prepare structured answers

Use [STAR method answers](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/star-method-interview-answers-guide) for every behavioral question. Panelists compare notes afterward - consistent, structured answers stand out.

### Practice with mock interviews

Run sessions on the [AI mock interview tool](https://thetailorcv.com/mock-interview) focusing on clarity and brevity - panel interviews have less tolerance for rambling.

### Prepare questions for each person

"What does success look like in the first 90 days?" for the manager. "How does the team handle code review?" for the peer. See [questions to ask in an interview](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/questions-to-ask-in-an-interview).

## During the Panel Interview

### Address the whole panel, then focus on the asker

When answering:
1. Make brief eye contact with everyone when you start
2. Focus on the person who asked the question
3. Glance at others during key points to include them

Read [interview body language tips](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/interview-body-language-communication-tips) for virtual panels too - [remote interview prep](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/remote-job-interview-preparation-2026) applies.

### Keep answers concise

With multiple interviewers, time is tighter. Aim for 90 seconds to 2 minutes. Practice trimming with [tell me about yourself](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-answer-tell-me-about-yourself) timing.

### Take notes

Write down names and roles when introduced. Reference specific panelists when asking questions: "Sarah, you mentioned the migration project - could you tell me more about the team's approach?"

### Stay calm when interrupted

Panelists may jump in with follow-ups. Pause, listen, and respond directly. Manage stress with [interview anxiety tips](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/interview-anxiety-nervousness-tips).
