ChatGPT can genuinely improve a resume - if you know what to ask for. Vague prompts like "make my resume better" produce vague, generic output. Specific prompts targeting one section at a time produce something you can actually use. Here are 15 prompts that work, organized by what they fix.

The catch: generic AI chat doesn't know your ATS score against a specific job, and it can't verify your resume is accurate. For that, pair these prompts with the [free ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions), which is purpose-built to score your resume against a real job description.

## Summary and Headline Prompts

1. "Rewrite this resume summary in 3 lines, focused on [target role], without generic phrases like 'hardworking' or 'team player': [paste summary]"
2. "Write 3 alternative resume headlines for a [target role] with [X years] of experience in [industry]"
3. "Turn this list of skills into a 2-sentence resume summary that sounds specific, not generic: [paste skills]"

See [how to write a resume summary](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-summary) and [how to write a resume headline](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-headline) for the structure these prompts should follow.

## Bullet Point Prompts

4. "Rewrite this bullet point to lead with a measurable result: [paste bullet]"
5. "Turn this task description into a resume bullet using the format: action verb + what you did + measurable outcome: [paste task]"
6. "Suggest 3 stronger action verbs to replace 'responsible for' in this bullet: [paste bullet]"

Pair these with [how to write resume bullet points](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-bullet-points), [best action verbs for resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/best-action-verbs-for-resume), and [how to quantify resume achievements](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-resume-achievements).

## Keyword and Job-Matching Prompts

7. "List the top 10 hard skills and keywords mentioned in this job description: [paste JD]"
8. "Compare this resume to this job description and list which required skills are missing: [paste both]"
9. "Suggest natural ways to include [specific keyword] in a resume bullet without keyword stuffing"

These prompts approximate what the [free ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions) does automatically and more precisely - see [how AI resume matching works](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-ai-resume-matching-works) for why a purpose-built matching tool outperforms manual prompting here.

## Formatting and Structure Prompts

10. "List which of these resume section headers are ATS-friendly and which might cause parsing issues: [paste headers]"
11. "Suggest a logical section order for a resume for a [career changer / fresher / senior executive]"

See [ATS resume formatting mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes) and [resume section order guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-section-order-guide) for what a well-structured answer should include.

## Career-Specific Prompts

12. "Rewrite my resume summary for a career change from [old field] to [new field], emphasizing transferable skills"
13. "Suggest 5 project ideas that would strengthen a resume for a [target role] with no formal experience"
14. "Write 3 versions of a resume bullet for someone returning to work after a career break, focused on recent skills"

## Where Generic ChatGPT Prompts Fall Short

- **No real ATS score.** ChatGPT can guess at keyword overlap, but it can't give you an actual match percentage against applicant tracking systems the way a [dedicated ATS checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions) can.
- **No memory of your full resume across a whole rewrite.** You end up pasting sections repeatedly and losing consistency.
- **No built-in tailoring workflow.** You have to manually copy suggestions back into your resume every time - see [how to tailor your resume for every job](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-tailor-resume-for-every-job) for a faster, structured alternative.
- **Risk of sounding generic.** Left unedited, AI-generated phrasing has recognizable patterns - see [AI resume tailoring without losing your voice](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ai-resume-tailoring-human-voice).
