Supply chain is highly measurable - inventory turns, on-time delivery, cost savings, and lead time reductions tell your story. Your resume should be built around these metrics.

## Supply Chain Manager Resume Summary

Formula:

Supply Chain Manager with X years of experience in [procurement / logistics / planning / operations]. Managed [budget or volume]. Delivered [cost savings, efficiency, or delivery improvement].

### Example for Experienced Supply Chain Manager

Supply Chain Manager with 7 years of experience in FMCG procurement and logistics. Managed a $40M procurement budget and a network of 30+ suppliers. Reduced logistics costs by 18%, improved on-time delivery from 82% to 96%, and increased inventory turns from 6 to 9. Certified in APICS CSCP and Lean Six Sigma.

### Example for Entry-Level Supply Chain Professional

Supply Chain Analyst with 2 years of experience in demand planning and inventory management. Reduced stockouts by 24% and improved forecast accuracy through data-driven planning. Proficient in SAP, Excel, and Power BI. Seeking a supply chain coordinator or manager role.

## Supply Chain Manager Core Competencies

Planning: Demand planning, S&OP, inventory management, forecasting, capacity planning, MRP
Procurement: Supplier management, sourcing, contract negotiation, vendor evaluation, cost reduction
Logistics: Transportation management, warehousing, distribution, freight optimization, last-mile delivery
Systems: SAP, Oracle SCM, Blue Yonder, Kinaxis, Excel, Power BI, WMS, TMS
Improvement: Lean, Six Sigma, process optimization, KPI management
Risk: Supply chain risk management, contingency planning, supplier diversification

## How to Write Supply Chain Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Reduced / Improved / Managed / Negotiated + [supply chain process] + [scope or volume] + [cost, delivery, inventory, or efficiency outcome]

### Strong Bullet Points

- Reduced logistics costs by 18% ($2.4M annually) by optimizing freight routing, consolidating shipments, and renegotiating carrier contracts.
- Improved on-time delivery from 82% to 96% over 12 months by redesigning the demand planning process and implementing real-time order tracking.
- Increased inventory turns from 6 to 9 and reduced excess inventory by $3.1M through demand-driven replenishment and SKU rationalization.
- Negotiated contracts with 12 key suppliers, achieving 14% average cost reduction while improving payment terms and lead times.
- Led an S&OP process implementation across sales, finance, and operations, improving forecast accuracy from ±18% to ±6%.

## Supply Chain Manager Resume Example

Supply Chain Manager
FMCG Company | Apr 2021 - Present

- Manage end-to-end supply chain for a $200M product portfolio, overseeing procurement, planning, logistics, and a 25-person team.
- Reduced total supply chain cost by 15% ($6M annually) through freight optimization, supplier consolidation, and warehouse layout redesign.
- Improved on-time-in-full (OTIF) delivery from 79% to 94% by implementing a demand-driven planning system and supplier scorecards.
- Led supplier risk diversification after a major disruption, qualifying 8 alternate suppliers and reducing single-source dependency from 40% to 12%.
- Implemented a Power BI supply chain dashboard tracking 15 KPIs, enabling weekly data-driven decisions across the leadership team.

## Common Supply Chain Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No metrics

Supply chain is measurable. Show cost savings, OTD/OTIF, inventory turns, lead time, and forecast accuracy.

### Mistake 2: Listing functions without outcomes

"Managed procurement and logistics" is a job description. Show what you improved and saved.

### Mistake 3: No systems listed

SAP, Oracle, Blue Yonder, Kinaxis - list your specific systems. These are screening filters.

### Mistake 4: No improvement methodology

Lean, Six Sigma, and S&OP experience are valued. Include them if you have them.
