## Key Takeaways

- A BI analyst resume must show the decision a dashboard changed, not just that the dashboard exists.
- Name the BI tool the ad uses (Power BI, Tableau, Looker) and its parts - DAX, Power Query, LOD expressions, LookML.
- SQL and data modelling (star schema, dimensional modelling) are in nearly every BI job ad. Give them their own bullets.
- Show adoption: how many people use your reports, and what time or money they saved.

A business intelligence analyst resume gets interviews when it connects data work to business decisions. Recruiters scan for the BI tool, SQL depth, data modelling and the impact of your reports. "Built dashboards in Power BI" is a task; "Built a Power BI sales dashboard used by 120 managers that replaced 6 manual Excel reports" is a result.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Develop dashboards in Power BI / Tableau" | Name the tool, the audience and the decision the dashboard supports. |
| "Advanced SQL" | A bullet with complex queries, optimisation or data volume. |
| "Data modelling and ETL" | Star schema, dbt, SSIS, Azure Data Factory - whatever you used. |
| "Partner with business stakeholders" | Who you worked with and what changed for them. |

## Tailoring Walkthrough

- **Power BI-heavy ad:** lead with DAX, data modelling and refresh automation.
- **Tableau-heavy ad:** lead with Tableau dashboards and calculated fields, keeping Power BI in skills.
- **Data-analyst-style ad:** move analysis and insight bullets above tooling - compare with the [data analyst resume guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/data-analyst-resume-2026).

## Common Mistakes

1. **Listing every chart type you know.** Show business outcomes instead.
2. **No SQL bullet.** BI roles are SQL roles - prove it.
3. **Screenshots of dashboards in the resume.** Images break ATS parsing; link a portfolio instead.
4. **Generic "data-driven" phrasing.** Name the decision you informed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a BI analyst and a data analyst resume?
A BI analyst resume centres on reporting systems - dashboards, data models, automation and adoption. A data analyst resume centres on analysis and insights. Many roles mix both; lead with whichever the job ad stresses.

### Should I include a dashboard portfolio?
Yes, if you can share public or anonymised work. Link it in your header rather than adding images to the resume.

### Which certification helps a BI analyst resume?
Microsoft PL-300 (Power BI Data Analyst) and Tableau certifications are the most recognised. List them near the top if the ad mentions the tool.
