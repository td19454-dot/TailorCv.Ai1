## Key Takeaways

- CSM hiring managers want three numbers: retention (or churn), net revenue retention, and product adoption or health-score improvement.
- Show onboarding: time to value is one of the most-watched CS metrics, and a strong onboarding bullet stands out.
- Name the CS platform (Gainsight, ChurnZero, Totango) and your CRM - they are common ATS filters.
- Be clear on whether you were commercial (owned renewals or upsell) or advisory. The job ad will tell you which matters.

A customer success manager resume gets interviews when it proves customers stayed and grew because of you. That means churn and retention numbers, adoption gains and a clear picture of your book. Bullets like "built strong customer relationships" appear on every CSM resume and prove nothing.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Own a portfolio of customers" | Number of accounts, ARR and segment. |
| "Reduce churn and drive renewals" | Gross retention or churn rate, ideally before/after. |
| "Drive adoption and expansion" | Usage or health-score gains and expansion revenue. |
| "Onboard new customers" | Time to value or onboarding time reduced. |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Relationship language with no numbers.** Retention and adoption are measurable - use them.
2. **Describing support tickets as customer success.** Show proactive work, not reactive fixes.
3. **No book size.** Recruiters can't judge your level without accounts and ARR.
4. **Hiding the tools.** Gainsight, Salesforce and usage analytics are frequent filters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important metric on a CSM resume?
Retention - gross revenue retention, net revenue retention or churn. If you only include one number, make it that.

### Can I move from customer support to customer success?
Yes. Rewrite your support experience around proactive outcomes: customers you onboarded, adoption you improved, churn risks you flagged. Those are CSM skills.

### Should a CSM resume mention product knowledge?
Briefly. Name the product type and its users, and give one example of using product data to change a customer's outcome.
