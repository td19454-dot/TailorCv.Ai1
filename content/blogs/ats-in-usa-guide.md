## What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers to manage the recruitment process. It automates the sorting and filtering of resumes based on specific criteria, such as keywords, skills, and formatting. Understanding how ATS evaluates resumes is essential for job seekers aiming to increase their visibility and improve their chances of getting hired.

### How ATS Works

1. **Parsing**: When a resume is submitted, the ATS parses the information, extracting relevant details such as contact information, work history, education, and skills.
2. **Ranking**: The system ranks resumes based on how well they match the job description. This ranking is often influenced by keyword usage and the overall format of the resume.
3. **Filtering**: Resumes that don’t meet the minimum criteria set by the employer are filtered out, meaning they never reach the hiring manager's desk.

## Why is ATS Important for Job Seekers?

Understanding ATS is vital for several reasons:

- **Increased Competition**: With many applicants for each job, ATS helps employers quickly identify the most qualified candidates.
- **Keyword Optimization**: Job descriptions often contain specific keywords that ATS looks for, making it essential to tailor your resume accordingly.
- **Format Compatibility**: Many [ATS systems](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-an-ats-score-and-why-does-it-decide-your-job-application-before-any-human-reads-it) struggle with complex formatting, so using a straightforward design can prevent your resume from being misread.

## Crafting an ATS-Friendly Resume

To beat the ATS and improve your chances of landing a job, follow these guidelines for creating an [ATS-friendly resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-parser-friendly-format):

### 1. Use Standard Formatting

- **Avoid Graphics and Images**: ATS may not read these elements correctly.
- **Simple Fonts**: Stick to standard fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman.
- **Consistent Headings**: Use standard headings like "Work Experience" and "Education."

### 2. Incorporate Relevant Keywords

- **Analyze Job Descriptions**: Identify keywords and phrases that appear frequently in job listings for your target position.
- **Use Variations**: Include variations of keywords to capture different ways employers might phrase the same requirements.

### 3. Optimize Bullet Points

- **Action Verbs**: Start bullet points with strong action verbs (e.g., "Developed," "Managed," "Increased").
- **Quantify Achievements**: Use numbers to showcase your accomplishments (e.g., "Increased sales by 30% in six months").

**Example**:

Instead of saying:
- "Responsible for managing a team."

Say:
- "Led a team of 5 to achieve a 20% increase in project efficiency over six months."

### 4. Tailor Your Resume for Each Job

- **Customize Content**: Modify your resume for each application to align with the specific job description.
- **Use Job Title**: Incorporate the exact job title in your resume to ensure it matches the [ATS criteria](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/does-my-resume-pass-ats).

### 5. Keep It One Page (if Early-Career)

For early-career candidates, a one-page resume is often preferred. Focus on relevant experiences and skills that align with the job you're applying for.

## Common ATS Pitfalls to Avoid

Navigating the intricacies of Applicant Tracking Systems can be challenging. Here are some common mistakes job seekers make and how to avoid them:

1. **Ignoring Job Descriptions**: Many candidates fail to tailor their resumes to match specific job descriptions. Always extract keywords and phrases from the job listing and incorporate them naturally into your resume.

2. **Using Uncommon File Formats**: While PDF files are generally safe, some ATS struggle to read them. Opt for .docx or .txt formats unless specified otherwise in the job application.

3. **Overloading with Graphics**: Avoid using images, graphics, or unusual fonts. ATS may not recognize these elements, which can lead to your resume being misread or discarded.

4. **Including Irrelevant Information**: Focus on experience and skills that are relevant to the job you're applying for. Extraneous information can dilute your application and confuse the ATS.

5. **Neglecting the Cover Letter**: Some candidates believe a cover letter is unnecessary. However, many ATS also scan cover letters for keywords, so don’t skip this vital component. Use our [cover letter tool](https://www.thetailorcv.com/cover-letter) to ensure your letter is ATS-friendly.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Crafting an ATS-Friendly Resume

Creating an [ATS-friendly resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-keyword-mistakes) does not have to be overwhelming. Follow this step-by-step guide to ensure your application gets past the ATS and into the hands of hiring managers:

1. **Choose the Right Format**: Start with a simple, clean format. Use standard section headings like "Work Experience," "Education," and "Skills" to help the ATS easily categorize your information.

2. **Identify Relevant Keywords**: Analyze the job description and highlight key skills, qualifications, and industry-specific terms. Use tools like our [ATS Keywords](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-keywords-to-boost-score) guide to discover effective keywords.

3. **Incorporate Keywords Naturally**: Integrate the identified keywords throughout your resume, particularly in the skills and experience sections. Ensure they flow naturally within your descriptions.

4. **Use Standard Job Titles**: If your previous job title was unique, consider using a more common title in parentheses next to yours. This will help the ATS recognize your experience.

5. **Save and Submit**: When saving your resume, use a clear filename that includes your name and the job title. For example, "John_Doe_Marketing_Manager_Resume.docx" will help you stay organized and make a good impression.

## Do's and Don'ts for ATS Optimization

When crafting your resume, keep these do's and don'ts in mind:

### Do's:

- **Do use standard fonts**: Stick to common fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman.
- **Do customize your resume**: Tailor your resume for each job application to align with the specific role.
- **Do test your resume with an ATS checker**: Use tools like the [ATS Resume Checker](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-checker-how-it-works) to see how well your resume performs.

### Don'ts:

- **Don't use tables or columns**: These can confuse the ATS, leading to misinterpretation of your information.
- **Don't include unnecessary personal details**: Avoid including your age, marital status, or any other personal information that isn't relevant to the job.
- **Don't forget to update your LinkedIn**: Ensure your LinkedIn profile is aligned with your resume, as recruiters often cross-reference both.

By following these guidelines, you can significantly improve your chances of passing through ATS filters and landing job interviews. Remember, a well-optimized resume is your first step towards a successful job search.
