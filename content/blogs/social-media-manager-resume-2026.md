A social media manager resume in 2026 must prove that you grow audiences and drive measurable business results across platforms — not just that you post content. Social media is highly measurable: follower growth, engagement rate, reach, conversions, and revenue attribution tell the real story. Your resume should be built around these numbers.

## Best Social Media Manager Resume Format

1. Header with portfolio / handles links
2. Summary with a key growth metric
3. Skills by platform and function
4. Work experience
5. Education and certifications

One to two pages.

## Social Media Manager Resume Summary

Formula:

Social Media Manager with X years of experience growing [platforms] for [brand type]. Grew [followers, engagement, or reach] and drove [leads, sales, or conversions].

### Example for Experienced Social Media Manager

Social Media Manager with 4 years of experience managing organic and paid social for consumer and B2B brands. Grew a brand's Instagram following from 18K to 140K in 14 months and increased engagement rate from 1.8% to 4.6%. Drove $280K in attributed revenue through social commerce campaigns. Expert in content strategy, community management, and social analytics.

### Example for Entry-Level Social Media Manager

Social Media Coordinator with 2 years of experience managing Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn for small businesses. Created content calendars, grew accounts organically, and increased average engagement by 60%. Proficient in Canva, CapCut, Later, and Meta Business Suite. Seeking a social media manager role.

## How to Write Social Media Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Grew / Created / Managed / Launched + [platform or campaign] + [content or audience context] + [follower, engagement, reach, or revenue result]

### Strong Bullet Points

- Grew brand Instagram following from 22K to 130K in 12 months through a consistent Reels strategy, increasing average engagement rate from 1.9% to 4.8%.
- Launched a TikTok content strategy from zero to 85K followers in 8 months, with 3 videos surpassing 1M views each.
- Managed $120K annual paid social budget across Meta and TikTok, achieving a 4.3x ROAS on social commerce campaigns.
- Created and managed a content calendar producing 20+ posts weekly across 4 platforms, maintaining consistent brand voice and a 95% on-schedule publishing rate.
- Built an influencer collaboration program with 30 micro-influencers, generating 2.4M impressions and 1,200 trackable conversions in one quarter.

## Common Social Media Manager Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No growth metrics

Follower growth, engagement rate, reach, and revenue are your core credentials. A social resume without numbers looks like a hobby.

### Mistake 2: Listing platforms without results

"Managed Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn" is incomplete. Show what you grew and achieved on each.

### Mistake 3: No revenue or conversion link

The strongest social resumes connect social activity to business outcomes — leads, sales, traffic, conversions.

### Mistake 4: No portfolio

Link to accounts you have grown or a portfolio of your content and campaign results.
