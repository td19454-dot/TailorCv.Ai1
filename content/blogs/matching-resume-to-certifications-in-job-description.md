## First, Figure Out How "Required" the Certification Really Is

Not all certification requirements carry the same weight. Ask three questions:

1. **Is it listed as required or preferred?** Read [required vs preferred qualifications](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/matching-resume-required-vs-preferred-qualifications) to learn how to tell the difference.
2. **Is it legally or contractually mandatory?** Certifications tied to safety, compliance, or licensing (a nursing license, a bar admission, a CPA for signing audits) are usually hard blockers. Certifications tied to best practice or preference (many project management or cloud certifications) are often flexible.
3. **Is it achievable quickly?** Some certifications can be completed in weeks. Others take months or years. This affects whether "in progress" is a credible claim.

## How to Handle a Missing Certification on Your Resume

### If You Are Actively Pursuing It

State this clearly and specifically: "PMP Certification - In Progress, Exam Scheduled [Month Year]" is far stronger than leaving it off entirely or vaguely implying you have it. Never state a certification as complete if it is not.

### If You Have Equivalent Experience Without the Formal Credential

Lead with the experience itself. If a posting wants a Scrum Master certification and you have run agile teams for three years without the formal credential, make the experience unmistakable in your bullets, and consider a brief note acknowledging the certification gap in your [cover letter](/cover-letter).

### If the Certification Is a Hard Legal Requirement

Be honest. Do not apply to roles requiring an active license or credential you do not hold and cannot obtain before the expected start date. This wastes your time and the employer's, and following [job description analysis](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-analysis-checklist) closely will help you filter these out earlier.

## Should You Apply Without the Certification?

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|-----------------|
| Certification is listed as "preferred" | Apply. Focus your resume on matching every other requirement strongly. |
| Certification is listed as "required" but you have 3-5+ years of equivalent hands-on experience | Apply, and address the gap directly and briefly in your cover letter. |
| Certification is a legal or safety requirement (license, compliance credential) | Do not apply unless you can obtain it before the expected start date. |
| Certification is achievable within weeks and you are already studying | Apply, and list it as "in progress" with an expected completion date. |

Read [resume tailoring for underqualified candidates](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-tailoring-underqualified) for a broader framework on applying to roles where you do not meet every listed qualification.

## Closing the Gap Fast

If a certification keeps appearing across postings you want to apply to, it is worth prioritizing:

## How to Word "In Progress" Certifications Correctly

Vague or overstated claims about certification status can backfire, especially at companies that verify credentials before an offer.

**Weak:** "PMP Certified" (when you are not)

**Better:** "PMP Certification - In Progress, Exam Scheduled March 2026"

**Also acceptable:** "Completed PMP exam prep coursework; certification exam scheduled for [Month Year]"

Precision here builds credibility rather than undermining it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I lie about having a certification I don't actually have?

No. Certification claims are frequently verified, and misrepresenting your credentials can disqualify you immediately or, worse, cost you a job offer after the fact. Always represent your status accurately.

### Is it worth applying if I'm missing a "required" certification?

Often yes, if the certification is not tied to a legal or safety requirement and you have strong equivalent experience. Missing a certification listed as required but functionally treated as preferred is common, and many hiring managers will still consider strong candidates. For more insights, check out our [Job Description Analysis Checklist Before You Apply](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-analysis-checklist).

### How do I list a certification I'm currently studying for?

State it clearly as "in progress" with an expected completion or exam date, so it reads as a credible, active pursuit rather than an unfinished claim. You can also use our [Resume Keyword Map Template - Match Your Resume to Any Job Description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-keyword-map-template) to enhance your resume.

### Does a missing certification always hurt my ATS score significantly?

It depends on how the employer configured their screening. A single missing certification among many strong matches usually has a smaller impact than several missing core requirements. Check your resume's overall match score for context using our [Free ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions).

### How do I know which certifications are worth pursuing?

Track which certifications repeatedly appear across job descriptions in your target roles, and prioritize the ones that show up most often or are explicitly required rather than preferred.
