Before you send a single application, run your portfolio through this checklist. Five minutes here prevents the silent rejections that come from a dead link, a slow page, or a missing contact path. Pair this with [Portfolio Mistakes to Avoid](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-mistakes-to-avoid) and [How to Build a Professional Portfolio](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-build-professional-portfolio).

## Content
- [ ] 3–5 strong projects; weak ones removed ([15 Developer Portfolio Project Ideas](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/developer-portfolio-project-ideas))
- [ ] Each project has problem, process, and outcome ([How to Write a Portfolio Case Study](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-portfolio-case-study))
- [ ] Real metrics or proxies where possible ([How to Quantify Resume Achievements](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-resume-achievements))
- [ ] A clear, specific [Portfolio About Me Section](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-about-me-section)
- [ ] No confidential or NDA material

## Links & Technical
- [ ] Every demo and repo link works (test in incognito)
- [ ] Loads fast — images compressed ([How to Host Your Portfolio for Free](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-host-portfolio-free))
- [ ] Looks great on mobile (test at 320px)
- [ ] HTTPS enabled
- [ ] Resume link present and current ([How to Add Your Portfolio Link to Your Resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/add-portfolio-link-to-resume))

## Contact & CTA
- [ ] Obvious contact path ([Portfolio Contact Section](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-contact-section))
- [ ] LinkedIn / GitHub links correct ([GitHub Portfolio Optimization](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/github-portfolio-optimization))
- [ ] A clear call to action

## Findability
- [ ] Title tag with your name + role ([Portfolio SEO: Get Found](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-seo-get-found))
- [ ] Meta description set
- [ ] Indexed (not blocked by robots.txt)
- [ ] Custom domain connected ([Portfolio Domain Name Guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-domain-name-guide))

## Consistency
- [ ] Same name, headline, and story as your resume and LinkedIn
- [ ] Resume optimized via the [ATS checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions) and an [ATS-friendly template](https://www.thetailorcv.com/templates)

## Final Pass
- [ ] A friend reviewed it on their phone
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked

Fix any gaps fast by rebuilding with the [portfolio builder](https://thetailorcv.com/portfolio).

## Keep Your Resume and Portfolio in Sync

Your resume, your LinkedIn, and your portfolio should tell the **same** story — same name, same headline, same top projects — just at different levels of depth. A recruiter who sees a 'Full Stack Developer' resume and a portfolio headlined 'Aspiring Designer' gets confused, and confusion loses interviews. Lock the resume down first with the [ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions) and an [ATS-friendly template](https://www.thetailorcv.com/templates), then mirror that exact positioning in your portfolio. When they reinforce each other, every recruiter touchpoint pushes you forward. See [How to Add Your Portfolio Link to Your Resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/add-portfolio-link-to-resume) for placing the link correctly.



## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I run this checklist?

Before every job-search push and whenever you add a project. Links rot — re-test every cycle.

### What is the most commonly missed item?

Dead links and slow mobile performance. Both quietly cost interviews.

### My portfolio fails several items — what now?

Rebuild quickly from your resume with the [portfolio builder](https://thetailorcv.com/portfolio) and re-run the list.
