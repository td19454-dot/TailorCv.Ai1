## Key Takeaways

- **Bengaluru is India's technology capital** by a clear margin — product companies, startups, venture capital and the deepest engineering talent pool.
- **Hyderabad has grown into a genuine second hub**, with very large global capability centres, a strong pharmaceutical sector and lower costs.
- **The employer types differ sharply**: startups, Indian product companies, global product companies, GCCs and IT services all hire differently.
- **Hyderabad is frequently the better value proposition** — comparable pay at several large employers with meaningfully lower living costs.
- **Both cities hire heavily off-campus**, so college tier matters less here than at mass campus recruiters.

## Bengaluru

The centre of gravity for Indian technology, and the reasons are cumulative rather than accidental.

**Product companies.** Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato's engineering presence, PhonePe, Razorpay, Zerodha, Meesho, CRED and a long list besides. These are consumer-scale systems with genuine engineering problems.

**SaaS.** Freshworks, Zoho's presence, Postman, Chargebee and a substantial business-software cluster.

**Global product companies.** Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Salesforce, Uber, Walmart Global Tech and others run large engineering organisations here doing core product work rather than support.

**Startups and venture capital.** The densest ecosystem in India by a wide margin, with the funding infrastructure to match.

**Research.** IISc and the R&D arms of several multinationals.

**The trade-offs are real.** Bengaluru's traffic is genuinely difficult, housing costs are the highest among Indian technology cities, and the infrastructure has not kept pace with growth.

## Hyderabad

The fastest-growing alternative and increasingly a first choice rather than a fallback.

**Very large global capability centres.** Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Salesforce, Goldman Sachs, Wells Fargo, Deloitte and many others operate substantial Hyderabad centres, several among their largest outside their home countries.

**HITEC City and the Financial District** concentrate this employment.

**Pharmaceuticals and life sciences** are a genuine Hyderabad specialism, with Dr Reddy's, Aurobindo and a large manufacturing and research base — which also means bioinformatics and health technology roles.

**A growing startup scene**, smaller than Bengaluru's but real.

**Infrastructure is better.** Hyderabad's roads, metro and planned development compare favourably with Bengaluru, and this is a genuine daily-life difference rather than a marginal one.

**Costs are lower**, particularly housing, which for the same salary produces a materially different standard of living.

## Employer types and how they hire

| Type | How they hire | What they pay |
|---|---|---|
| **Early startups** | Referrals, direct outreach, fast processes | Variable, sometimes equity-heavy |
| **Indian product companies** | Portals and referrals, DSA-heavy interviews | Strong |
| **Global product companies** | Structured processes, algorithmic interviews | Highest |
| **GCCs** | Own portals, campus and off-campus | Strong, above services |
| **IT services** | Mass campus drives, aptitude tests | Standardised, modest |

**The interview preparation differs.** Global product companies and Indian product companies both run algorithmic and system design interviews. GCCs vary — some mirror their parent's global process, others are closer to a conventional technical interview. Services companies test aptitude and basics.

**Referrals matter substantially** at product companies and startups, and less at services recruiters where the process is standardised.

**Off-campus hiring is the norm** at all of these except the mass services recruiters, which is why these cities are accessible regardless of which college you attended.

## Scenarios

### Scenario 1: You want the deepest startup ecosystem

Bengaluru, without much doubt. The density of product companies, startups and venture funding is not matched anywhere else in India, and the network effects are real.

### Scenario 2: You want strong pay with a better standard of living

Hyderabad. Several of the largest global centres pay comparably to Bengaluru while housing and daily costs are meaningfully lower, and the infrastructure is genuinely better.

### Scenario 3: You are from a tier-2 or tier-3 college

Both cities hire heavily off-campus. Build projects, prepare DSA properly, target GCCs and product companies directly, and use referrals. College tier is a much weaker filter here than at mass campus recruiters.

> **Tip:** Compare Bengaluru and Hyderabad on rent and commute rather than on reputation. Several global centres pay similarly in both cities, and the difference in housing cost and daily travel time is large enough to change your actual quality of life substantially.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Match preparation to the employer type
- Consider Hyderabad seriously against Bengaluru
- List GCC employers by name
- Use referrals for product companies
- Deploy and link your projects
- Compare cities on real living costs

### Don't

- Assume Bengaluru is automatically the right choice
- Prepare generically across employer types
- Ignore GCCs
- Rely on college tier
- Underestimate Bengaluru commute times
- Skip system design if targeting senior product roles

## Common Mistakes

- **Bengaluru by default.** Hyderabad frequently offers comparable pay with better daily life.
- **Generic interview preparation.** Product, GCC and services interviews test different things.
- **Ignoring GCCs.** Large, well paid and consistently under-targeted.
- **No referral effort.** At product companies and startups this matters substantially.
- **Underestimating commute.** In Bengaluru it materially affects where you should live.
- **Assuming college tier blocks you.** Off-campus hiring in both cities is extensive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bangalore still the best city for tech jobs in India?

By depth of ecosystem, yes — product companies, startups and venture capital are concentrated there. Hyderabad has closed much of the gap on employment volume and offers better infrastructure and lower costs.

### What is Hyderabad strong in?

Very large global capability centres, pharmaceuticals and life sciences, and a growing startup scene, concentrated around HITEC City and the Financial District.

### Do these cities hire off-campus?

Extensively. Outside mass services recruitment, most hiring in both cities happens off-campus, which makes them accessible regardless of college tier.

### How do interviews differ by employer type?

Product companies run algorithmic and system design interviews; GCCs vary between their parent's global process and a conventional technical interview; services companies test aptitude and fundamentals.

### Which city is better value?

Hyderabad, generally. Several large employers pay comparably while housing and daily costs are lower and infrastructure is better.

### Do referrals matter?

Substantially at product companies and startups, much less at mass services recruiters where the process is standardised and criteria-driven.
