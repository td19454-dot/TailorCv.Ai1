A photography portfolio sells two things at once: a feeling and a standard. Tight curation, smart sequencing, and fast galleries are what turn browsers into bookings. Pair this with [How to Build a Professional Portfolio](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-build-professional-portfolio) and [Graphic Designer Portfolio Guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/graphic-designer-portfolio-guide).

## Key Takeaways

- Curate your portfolio by focusing on a specific niche and include 20-40 strong images organized into clear galleries.
- Sequence your images to create a captivating flow, starting and ending with your best work while balancing different styles.
- Ensure your portfolio is technically optimized with fast-loading images, a clean layout, and mobile-first design.
- Choose a suitable platform for hosting your portfolio, considering user-friendliness and customization options.
- Keep your resume and portfolio aligned in terms of branding and messaging to avoid confusion for potential clients or employers.

## Curate by Intent
Show only the niche you want to be booked for. A wedding client should not have to scroll past product shots. Aim for 20-40 strong images, organized into clear galleries (e.g., Weddings, Portraits, Editorial). 

### Steps to Curate Effectively
1. **Identify Your Niche**: Determine what type of photography you want to focus on (e.g., weddings, portraits, commercial). 
2. **Select Your Best Work**: Choose images that not only showcase your skills but also resonate with your target audience.
3. **Use Feedback**: Share your selection with peers or mentors for constructive criticism.
4. **Revise Regularly**: Your portfolio should evolve. Regularly update it to reflect your latest work and style.

### Real Examples
- **Wedding Photographer**: A wedding photographer might include only images from the last two years that highlight different venues and styles, ensuring a cohesive narrative.
- **Commercial Photographer**: A commercial photographer could showcase campaigns that align with specific brands to attract similar clients.

## Sequence for Flow
Open strong, vary the rhythm, and close strong. Cut anything that breaks your standard - consistency is what signals professionalism and lets you charge more.

### Tips for Effective Sequencing
- **Start with Your Best Work**: The first image should captivate your audience immediately.
- **Balance Your Styles**: Alternate between different types of shots (e.g., wide shots, close-ups) to maintain interest.
- **Create a Narrative**: Sequence images to tell a story. For example, in a wedding portfolio, you might start with the venue, move to the ceremony, and finish with the reception.

## Nail the Technical Presentation
- Fast-loading, properly optimized images
- A clean, distraction-free layout
- **Mobile-first** (most clients browse on phones)
- An obvious booking/contact path ([Portfolio Contact Section](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-contact-section))

### Optimization Tips
- **Image Compression**: Use tools like TinyPNG or JPEGmini to reduce file sizes without sacrificing quality.
- **Responsive Design**: Test your portfolio on multiple devices to ensure it looks great everywhere.
- **Loading Speed**: Aim for a loading time of under three seconds. Use [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://developers.google.com/speed/pagespeed/insights/) to analyze and improve your site speed.

### Syncing Strategies
- **Use Consistent Language**: Ensure the descriptions in your resume match those in your portfolio.
- **Highlight Key Projects**: Choose 2-3 projects that illustrate your skills and include them in both documents.
- **Update Simultaneously**: When you make changes to your portfolio, do the same for your resume and LinkedIn.

## Common Mistakes
- Too many images and inconsistent quality
- Slow galleries that frustrate clients
- No clear pricing or contact path
- Dead links - run the [Portfolio Checklist Before You Apply](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-checklist-before-applying)

### Avoiding Pitfalls
- **Limit Your Selection**: Stick to your best work to maintain quality over quantity.
- **Test Your Site**: Regularly check for broken links and loading times.
- **Include Clear Calls to Action**: Make it easy for potential clients to reach out or book your services.
