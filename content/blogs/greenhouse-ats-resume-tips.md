## How Greenhouse Parses and Ranks Resumes

### Structured Data Extraction

Greenhouse extracts your work history, education, and skills into structured candidate profile fields, similar to most modern [ATS](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-score-guide) platforms. Clean, standard formatting parses far more reliably than heavily designed resumes.

### Keyword and Requirement Matching

Once parsed, your resume is compared against the [job description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-keyword-extraction-guide)'s listed requirements. Greenhouse is commonly configured by recruiting teams to flag or auto-sort candidates based on specific must-have keywords, so missing an exact required term can push you out of the initial review queue.

### Recruiter Scorecards

Unlike some platforms, Greenhouse gives recruiters and hiring managers structured "scorecards" to rate candidates against defined criteria. This means your resume needs to make it obvious, at a glance, that you meet each specific criterion the job posting lists, not just generally qualified.

## Formatting Rules for Greenhouse

| Rule | Why It Matters |
|------|-----------------|
| Keep formatting simple and single-column | Reduces parsing errors in the extraction engine |
| Use standard, recognizable section headers | Helps both the parser and the recruiter scan quickly |
| Lead bullets with strong action verbs and results | Startups often skim fast; the first few words matter most |
| Mirror the exact tool and skill names from the posting | Greenhouse-configured filters often search for exact strings |
| Keep the resume to 1-2 pages | Startup hiring teams review high volumes quickly |

Build from an [ATS-friendly resume template](/templates) to avoid formatting issues before they cost you a look from the hiring manager.

## Common Mistakes with Greenhouse Applications

### Assuming Startups Do Not Use ATS Filtering

Many candidates assume smaller companies read every resume manually. In reality, Greenhouse-powered hiring teams frequently use keyword filters and scorecards just like larger companies. Treat every Greenhouse application with the same rigor as a large enterprise one. Review [ATS keyword mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-keyword-mistakes) before you apply.

### Writing a Generic Summary

Startups hire for specific, immediate needs. A [generic resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-ats-detects-generic-resume) that could apply to any company signals a weak fit. Rewrite your summary for each role using the [resume summary matching guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-matching-with-job-description-complete-guide).

### Skipping the Cover Letter Field

Greenhouse applications often include an optional cover letter or "why this role" field. Leaving it blank is a missed opportunity to reinforce your match. Use the [AI cover letter generator](/cover-letter) to produce a tailored answer quickly.

### Not Matching Culture and Values Language

Startup job descriptions frequently include values-driven language ("scrappy," "ownership," "bias for action"). Reflecting this tone, where genuine, in your summary can strengthen your fit signal alongside your hard-skill keywords.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do startups using Greenhouse really filter resumes by keywords?

Yes. Many recruiting teams configure Greenhouse to flag or sort candidates using specific must-have keywords from the job posting, especially at high application volumes.

### Should I use a creative resume design for a startup application?

Keep it clean and simple. Creative layouts often parse poorly, and most Greenhouse-powered hiring teams still value clarity and scannability over design flair.

### What if the Greenhouse application has an optional cover letter field?

Fill it in. Optional fields are still read, and a tailored answer is a low-effort way to strengthen your match versus candidates who skip it.

### How is Greenhouse different from Workday for resume matching?

Greenhouse is typically used by startups and tech companies and relies heavily on recruiter scorecards, while [Workday](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/workday-resume-matching-guide) is used by larger enterprises with more rigid, multi-step application flows.
