## Key Takeaways

- A solutions architect resume must show decisions, not tasks: what you designed, which trade-off you made, and what it saved or enabled.
- Every strong bullet links a technical design to a business result - cost, revenue, time to market or risk.
- Pre-sales architects should show deal support (wins, deal size, proofs of concept); delivery architects should show systems built and their scale.
- Certifications (AWS Solutions Architect Professional, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Google Professional Cloud Architect) are common filters - list them high.

A solutions architect resume gets shortlisted when it reads like a record of good decisions. Hiring managers want to see that you can take a messy business problem, choose an architecture, explain the trade-offs to executives, and land it. Bullets that only list technologies look like an engineer's resume, not an architect's.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Design scalable, secure cloud architectures" | Name the platform and services, the scale, and one security decision. |
| "Engage with stakeholders and executives" | A bullet where you presented, persuaded or aligned leadership. |
| "Pre-sales / customer-facing" | Proof-of-concept wins, deal value, number of customers advised. |
| "Lead technical teams through delivery" | Team size and a delivered outcome. |

## Tailoring Walkthrough

- **Delivery / internal architect ad:** lead with systems you designed and their scale, cost and reliability results.
- **Pre-sales ad:** lead with customers, deals and proofs of concept, then show design depth.
- **Specialist ad (data, security):** move that domain to the first bullet and summary line.

## Common Mistakes

1. **A technology list with no decisions.** Architects are hired for judgement - show the trade-offs.
2. **No business numbers.** Cost, revenue and time saved are what executives read.
3. **Hiding hands-on depth.** Many teams want architects who can still prototype - say so if you can.
4. **Three pages of history.** Keep the last 10 years detailed and summarise earlier roles - see [how far back your resume should go](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-far-back-should-resume-go).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should a solutions architect resume be?
Two pages is normal for architects with 8+ years of experience. Keep the strongest architecture and business-impact bullets on page one.

### Do I need a cloud certification to be a solutions architect?
It isn't always required, but AWS, Azure and Google architect certifications appear in many job ads as filters or strong preferences. They also give recruiters a quick signal of your platform depth.

### How do I move from senior engineer to solutions architect on my resume?
Rewrite your bullets around decisions: designs you proposed, trade-offs you argued for, stakeholders you aligned. Many senior engineers already do architecture work but describe it as coding.
