## Key Takeaways

- Account manager resumes are judged on four numbers: book of business size, retention rate, revenue growth from existing accounts, and quota attainment.
- Name the accounts by size or type (enterprise, mid-market, SMB) and the industry - recruiters hire for similar books.
- Show expansion: upsell and cross-sell revenue is what separates account managers from support roles.
- Name your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) and keep the job ad's own terms, like "net revenue retention".

An account manager resume works when it reads like a revenue report. Hiring managers want to know how big a book you handled, how much of it you kept, and how much you grew it. Without those numbers, even good experience looks like customer service.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Manage a portfolio of key accounts" | Account count, total revenue and segment. |
| "Drive renewals and expansion" | Retention or renewal rate plus upsell revenue. |
| "Exceed quota" | Quota attainment by year (e.g. 118% of target). |
| "Build executive relationships" | A bullet where you worked with a C-level or VP contact. |

**Experienced:**

**Entry-level:**
Account coordinator moving into account management with 2 years supporting a 60-account SMB book. Ran renewals for 25 accounts at a 94% renewal rate and found $85K in upsell opportunities.

## Common Mistakes

1. **No book size.** Recruiters can't judge your level without it.
2. **Only activity metrics.** "50 calls a day" matters less than revenue kept and grown.
3. **Leaving out the industry.** A SaaS, agency or logistics book signals what you can step into.
4. **Calling it customer service.** Use the language of revenue ownership.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What numbers should an account manager resume include?
Book of business size, number of accounts, retention or renewal rate, upsell or expansion revenue and quota attainment. Even two of these make a big difference.

### Can I share client names on my resume?
Only if they are public and you're allowed to. Otherwise describe them by size and industry - "a Fortune 500 retailer" works well. See [confidential work on your resume](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/confidential-work-on-resume).

### What's the difference between an account manager and an account executive resume?
An account executive resume focuses on winning new business. An account manager resume focuses on keeping and growing existing accounts. Use the job ad's title in your headline.
