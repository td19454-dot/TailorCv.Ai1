## Common Fresher Interview Questions

### 1. Tell me about yourself

Keep it under 2 minutes. Focus on education, projects, and career goals. [Full guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-answer-tell-me-about-yourself).

### 2. Why should we hire you as a fresher?

Emphasize learning ability, fresh perspective, project experience, and enthusiasm. [Answer guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-answer-why-should-we-hire-you).

### 3. Tell me about your final year project

Use [STAR method](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/star-method-interview-answers-guide): your role, technologies, challenges, and outcome.

### 4. What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Real strengths with examples. Real weakness with improvement plan - [weakness guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-answer-greatest-weakness-interview).

### 5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Show ambition aligned with the company's growth path.

### 6. Are you willing to relocate / work in shifts?

Answer honestly based on your situation.

### 7. Do you have any questions for us?

Always yes - [questions to ask guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/questions-to-ask-in-an-interview).

## Fresher Interview Mistakes to Avoid

- Not knowing your own resume and project details
- Rambling in [tell me about yourself](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-answer-tell-me-about-yourself)
- No questions when asked
- Badmouthing college or professors
- Overclaiming skills you cannot demonstrate
- Not practicing at all - reading is not enough

See [20 common interview mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/common-interview-mistakes-to-avoid).
