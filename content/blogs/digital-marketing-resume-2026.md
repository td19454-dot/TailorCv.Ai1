## Digital Marketing Resume Summary

Formula:

Digital Marketer with X years of experience in [channels]. Managed [budgets or campaign scope] and grew [traffic, leads, ROAS, followers, or conversion rate] by [metric].

### Example for SEO Specialist

SEO Specialist with 4 years of experience growing organic search traffic for B2B SaaS and e-commerce brands. Grew organic monthly sessions from 12K to 68K across 3 clients through content strategy, technical SEO audits, and link building. Strong in Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console, and content brief writing.

### Example for Paid Ads Manager

Paid [digital marketing](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/digital-marketing-resume-2026) Manager with 3 years of experience managing Google Ads and Meta Ads for DTC e-commerce brands. Managed a combined $1.4M annual ad spend with average 4.2x ROAS. Expert in campaign structure, creative testing, audience segmentation, and attribution modeling.

## How to Write Digital Marketing Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Grew / Launched / Managed / Reduced + [channel or campaign] + [budget or audience scope] + [traffic, ROAS, leads, conversion, or growth outcome]

### Weak Bullet Points

- Managed Google Ads campaigns
- Did social media marketing
- Wrote blog posts for SEO
- Ran email campaigns

### Strong Bullet Points

- Grew organic search traffic from 9K to 51K monthly sessions in 14 months through a content strategy of 45 published articles targeting 120 high-intent keywords in the personal finance vertical.
- Managed $620K annual Google Ads budget across 8 product campaigns, achieving 4.4x ROAS and reducing CPA from $94 to $52 through bid strategy testing and negative keyword optimization.
- Built a Klaviyo email automation flow of 11 touchpoints for abandoned cart, welcome series, and post-purchase - generating $180K in attributed revenue in 6 months with 38% average open rate.
- Grew company [LinkedIn](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-use-linkedin-for-job-search) page from 4K to 26K followers in 9 months through daily content strategy, employee advocacy, and 2 viral posts reaching 300K+ impressions each.
- Reduced bounce rate from 72% to 48% on a key landing page through A/B testing headline, hero image, and CTA button changes - improving lead form submissions by 34%.

## Common Digital Marketing Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No performance metrics

[digital marketing](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/digital-marketing-resume-2026) is 100% measurable. If your resume has no ROAS, CPA, traffic numbers, conversion rates, or follower growth, it looks like you did not track your work.

### Mistake 2: Channels without results

"Managed Google Ads, Facebook Ads, SEO, email, and social media" is a tool list. Show what each channel produced.

### Mistake 3: Weak portfolio

Digital marketers should have case study links, Google Analytics screenshots, or campaign results documentation in their [portfolio](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-build-professional-portfolio). Reference it in your resume.

### Mistake 4: Outdated tools

In 2026, GA4, GA4 reports, and Google Ads Performance Max are standard. If your resume only mentions Universal Analytics or old ad formats, update it.

### How can I ensure my resume passes ATS screening?
To improve your chances of passing ATS screenings, use relevant keywords from the job description in your resume. ### What specific metrics should I include to demonstrate my digital marketing success?
Employers look for measurable results that showcase your impact. Include metrics such as organic traffic growth, conversion rates, or return on ad spend (ROAS).
