## What a Good Free ATS Checker Should Give You

Before comparing tools, know what "useful" actually looks like. A free ATS check should tell you, at minimum:

- Your overall [ATS match score](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-a-good-ats-score) against the specific job description
- Exactly which [keywords are missing](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/hidden-keywords-in-job-description), not just a vague percentage
- Whether your [formatting is parser-friendly](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes)
- A clear next step, not just a score with no explanation

If a "free" tool won't show you the missing keywords without paying, it isn't really free - it's a paywall with a teaser.

## How to Actually Use a Free ATS Score

A number alone doesn't get you hired. Once you have your score:

1. Fix the [ATS resume formatting mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes) that silently break parsing - tables, headers in text boxes, and images with important text.
2. Add the [missing keywords](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-keywords-to-boost-score) - but only where they're honestly true. See [ATS keyword mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-keyword-mistakes) to avoid keyword stuffing.
3. Re-run the check. Improvement should be measurable, not guessed. Use [how to increase ATS score](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-increase-ats-score) as your checklist.
4. Once your score is solid, move to [tailoring your resume for the specific role](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-tailor-resume-for-every-job) rather than sending the same version everywhere.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are free ATS checkers accurate?

Accuracy varies by tool and depends heavily on whether it's scoring against your actual target job description or a generic template. See [how AI resume matching works](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-ai-resume-matching-works) for how real matching should function.

### How many times should I re-check my resume?

Check once before applying to a new role, then again after every meaningful edit. Use the [resume review checklist](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-review-checklist-before-applying) so you're not re-scanning minor tweaks endlessly.

### What's a good ATS score?

Most guidance points to 80%+ match on the keywords that matter most for the role - read [what is a good ATS score](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-a-good-ats-score) for the full breakdown by industry.

### Do free checkers work for every resume format?

No - PDFs with complex formatting, tables, or graphics can confuse both the checker and the real ATS. Use an [ATS-friendly resume builder](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-friendly-resume-builder) if you're not sure your format is parser-safe.
