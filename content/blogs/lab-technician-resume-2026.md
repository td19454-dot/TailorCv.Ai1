## Key Takeaways

- **ASCP certification is the credential** — write it as ASCP writes it: MLT(ASCP) or MLS(ASCP).
- MLT and MLS are different scopes of practice. Do not blur them; postings screen on the difference.
- Department experience is the matching keyword: chemistry, haematology, microbiology, blood bank, molecular.
- Name the analyser platforms — Beckman, Siemens, Roche, Sysmex, Abbott. Postings do.
- Blood bank experience is the scarcest and most valuable specialisation in most markets.

Clinical laboratory hiring is department-specific and instrument-specific. A lab needs to know which benches you can run unsupervised and which analysers you have operated, because that determines your first-week utility.

## Best Lab Technician Resume Format for 2026

Reverse-chronological, one to two pages, single column. Avoid tables — hospital ATS installations parse them poorly, as covered in [ATS formatting mistakes](/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes).

Section order:

1. Header with credentials
2. Certification and licensure
3. Summary
4. Departments and instrumentation
5. Experience
6. Education

## Header and Certification

```
Grace Mensah, MLT(ASCP)
Houston, TX · (713) 555-0155 · g.mensah@email.com
```

Write ASCP credentials in their standard form. The distinction matters:

- **MLT(ASCP)** — Medical Laboratory Technician (associate-level)
- **MLS(ASCP)** — Medical Laboratory Scientist (bachelor-level; formerly MT)
- **SBB(ASCP)** — Specialist in Blood Banking
- **M(ASCP)** — Microbiology specialist
- **C(ASCP)** — Chemistry specialist
- **H(ASCP)** — Haematology specialist

```
CERTIFICATION & LICENSURE
Medical Laboratory Technician, MLT(ASCP) — certification #4429871, current through 2028
Texas Clinical Laboratory Personnel License #CLP-7742, active
Phlebotomy Technician PBT(ASCP) — 2022
```

Some states (California, New York, Florida among them) licence lab personnel separately. If yours does, list it — it is often a hard requirement.

## Department Experience Is the Matching Keyword

Labs hire for benches. Name yours explicitly with duration:

> **Chemistry** (4 yrs) · **Haematology** (4 yrs) · **Coagulation** (3 yrs) · **Urinalysis** (4 yrs) · **Blood Bank / Transfusion Services** (2 yrs) · **Microbiology** (1 yr) · **Molecular** (6 mo)

A generalist who can cover multiple benches is worth more than a single-department technician, particularly for night and weekend shifts. Say it plainly if it is true.

**Blood bank is the scarcest specialisation** in most markets. If you have transfusion service experience — type and screen, crossmatch, antibody identification, component preparation — put it high and be specific.

## Name Your Analysers

This is the equivalent of naming an EMR in clinical roles, and it is just as load-bearing:

> **Chemistry:** Beckman Coulter AU680 · Roche cobas 6000 · Siemens Atellica
> **Haematology:** Sysmex XN-1000 · Beckman DxH 800
> **Coagulation:** Stago STA-R · Werfen ACL TOP
> **Microbiology:** BD Phoenix · VITEK 2 · MALDI-TOF
> **Blood Bank:** Ortho Vision · Immucor Echo · gel and tube methods
> **LIS:** Epic Beaker · Sunquest · Cerner Millennium · SoftLab

Postings name platforms because retraining costs time. Matching them where true is straightforward [keyword work](/blog/how-to-match-resume-keywords-to-job-description).

## Professional Summary

> MLT(ASCP) with 4 years in a 24/7 hospital core lab processing 1,800+ specimens daily. Cross-trained across chemistry, haematology, coagulation and urinalysis; independent bench coverage on nights. Maintain 100% proficiency testing performance across all assigned CAP surveys.

## Best ATS Keywords for a Lab Technician Resume

**Technical:** specimen processing, venipuncture, phlebotomy, centrifugation, quality control, calibration, instrument maintenance, troubleshooting, reagent preparation, proficiency testing, delta checks, critical value reporting

**Compliance:** CLIA, CAP accreditation, Joint Commission, quality assurance, competency assessment, standard operating procedures, documentation, chain of custody, biosafety, PPE

**Departments:** clinical chemistry, haematology, coagulation, urinalysis, immunohematology, blood bank, transfusion services, microbiology, serology, molecular diagnostics

**Blood bank:** type and screen, crossmatch, antibody screen, antibody identification, component preparation, transfusion reaction workup, emergency release

## Writing Experience Bullets

**Weak:**
> Ran tests on laboratory samples and reported results.

**Strong:**
> Process and resulted 1,800+ specimens daily across chemistry, haematology and coagulation in a 24/7 core lab, with independent bench coverage on the night shift.

**Weak:**
> Performed quality control and maintenance.

**Strong:**
> Run daily QC and preventive maintenance on Sysmex XN-1000 and Beckman AU680; identified a reagent lot shift through delta-check review that prevented an estimated 200 erroneous results.

Numbers most lab techs have: specimens per shift, turnaround time, QC pass rate, proficiency testing scores, instruments maintained, critical values called, students trained. See [quantifying achievements](/blog/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume).

## Turnaround Time and QC Are Your Metrics

Labs are managed on TAT and quality. A resume speaking those terms speaks the manager's language:

> Reduced stat chemistry turnaround from 48 to 31 minutes by restructuring the centrifugation batch schedule, sustained across 12 months.

## Common Lab Technician Resume Mistakes

**Writing "ASCP certified" without the credential form.** MLT(ASCP) is the searchable string.

**Blurring MLT and MLS.** Different scopes; do not imply the higher one.

**Not naming departments.** Labs hire for specific benches.

**Omitting analyser platforms.** Retraining costs money; matching platforms is a real advantage.

**No specimen volume or TAT.** These are the operational facts of the job.

**Leaving out state licensure.** In licensure states it is a hard requirement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between MLT and MLS?**
Scope and education level — MLT is associate-level, MLS bachelor-level with broader independent responsibility. Write the one you hold.

**Do I need a state licence?**
In some states, yes. Where required it is a hard filter, so list it.

**Should I list every analyser?**
List platforms you have operated and maintained independently, weighted toward those the posting names.

**Is blood bank experience worth highlighting?**
Yes. It is the scarcest specialisation in most markets and frequently commands a premium.

**How long should the resume be?**
One page for new graduates; two once you cover several departments with instrumentation worth listing.

## Make This Practical

Lab postings name the department, the shift, the analysers and the certification. The usual reason a qualified technician gets filtered is an instrument or department they have covered but did not write in the posting's words.
