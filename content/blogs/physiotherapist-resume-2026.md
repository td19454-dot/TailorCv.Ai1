## Physiotherapist Resume Summary

Formula:

Licensed Physiotherapist with X years of experience in [orthopedic / neuro / sports / pediatric] rehabilitation. Skilled in [assessment, manual therapy, exercise prescription]. Helped [patient population] achieve [functional or recovery outcome].

### Example for Experienced Physiotherapist

Licensed Physiotherapist (MPT) with 6 years of experience in orthopedic and sports rehabilitation. Skilled in manual therapy, post-surgical rehabilitation, and movement assessment. Managed caseloads of 15+ patients daily, achieving above-benchmark functional recovery scores for ACL and rotator cuff rehabilitation programs.

### Example for Entry-Level Physiotherapist

Licensed Physiotherapist (BPT) with strong clinical assessment and exercise prescription skills. Completed 1,000+ supervised clinical hours across orthopedic, neurological, and cardiopulmonary settings. Seeking an outpatient physiotherapy role focused on musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

## Physiotherapist Skills

Assessment: Musculoskeletal assessment, gait analysis, range of motion (ROM) measurement, manual muscle testing, postural assessment, outcome measures (FIM, Berg Balance, VAS)
Treatment: Manual therapy, mobilization, soft tissue techniques, exercise prescription, electrotherapy, dry needling, taping
Specialties: Orthopedic, neurological (stroke, SCI, Parkinson's), sports, pediatric, geriatric, cardiopulmonary, post-surgical
Modalities: Ultrasound, TENS, IFT, hot/cold therapy, traction, hydrotherapy
Documentation: SOAP notes, treatment plans, progress reports, EMR systems
Patient Care: Patient education, home exercise programs, fall prevention, ergonomic advice

## How to Write Physiotherapist Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Assessed / Treated / Designed / Improved + [intervention or program] + [patient population] + [functional or recovery outcome]

### Strong Bullet Points

- Managed a daily caseload of 16 orthopedic patients, designing individualized rehabilitation programs that achieved an average 30% faster return-to-function versus standard recovery timelines.
- Led post-ACL reconstruction rehabilitation for 40+ athletes annually, with 88% returning to pre-injury sport level within the targeted 9-month timeline.
- Designed and implemented a fall prevention program for 50+ geriatric patients, reducing fall incidents by 34% over 6 months as measured by facility incident reports.
- Provided neurological rehabilitation for stroke patients, improving average Berg Balance Scale scores by 12 points over 8-week treatment courses.
- Educated 200+ patients on home exercise programs with documented adherence tracking, improving treatment adherence rates from 61% to 79%.

## Common Physiotherapist Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No patient outcomes

Recovery rates, return-to-sport percentages, functional score improvements, and fall reduction are powerful. Use them.

### Mistake 2: License not visible

Physiotherapy is licensed. Make your registration status clearly visible near the top.

### Mistake 3: Generic treatment descriptions

"Treated patients with various conditions" — specify your specialty, patient population, and the interventions you delivered.

### Mistake 4: No specialization clarity

Orthopedic, neuro, sports, pediatric — clarify your focus so employers can match you to the right setting.
