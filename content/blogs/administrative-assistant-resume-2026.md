## Key Takeaways

- Admin roles are full of measurable work - calendars, travel, invoices, meetings, phone volume. Count it.
- Software is a hard filter: Microsoft 365 (Excel, Outlook, Word), Google Workspace, and any scheduling, CRM or accounting tool the ad names.
- Show improvements, not just duties: a filing system, template or process that saved time is your strongest bullet.
- Tailor to the setting - medical, legal, school and corporate offices each use their own terms and software.

An administrative assistant resume gets interviews when it proves you keep an office running smoothly - and shows it with numbers. "Answered phones and scheduled meetings" describes every admin job. "Managed calendars for 5 managers and coordinated 40+ meetings a week" describes yours.

## What the job ad is really asking for

| Job ad says | What it means for your resume |
|---|---|
| "Proficient in Microsoft Office" | Name Excel, Outlook and Word, and show one Excel task (tracker, report). |
| "Manage calendars and schedule meetings" | How many people and meetings per week. |
| "Process invoices and expense reports" | Volume and any tool (QuickBooks, SAP Concur). |
| "Strong organisational skills" | A system you built or improved, with time saved. |

## Bullet Rewrites

**Before:** Answered phones and emails.
**After:** Handled 80+ calls and 120 emails a day as the first point of contact for a 60-person office.

**Before:** Scheduled meetings.
**After:** Managed calendars for 4 directors and coordinated 40+ internal and client meetings a week with no double bookings.

**Before:** Did filing.
**After:** Moved 6 years of paper files to a SharePoint folder system, cutting average document search time from 10 minutes to 2.

**Before:** Handled invoices.
**After:** Processed 150 supplier invoices a month in QuickBooks with a 99% on-time payment rate.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Only listing duties.** Add one improvement you made at each job.
2. **"Proficient in computers."** Name the software.
3. **No volume numbers.** Calls, meetings, invoices and appointments can all be counted.
4. **A decorative template.** Colourful two-column designs often fail ATS parsing - see [ATS formatting mistakes](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What skills should an administrative assistant list?
Microsoft 365 (especially Excel and Outlook), calendar management, data entry, invoicing or expenses, and communication. Add any software the job ad names, as long as you have used it.

### How do I write an admin assistant resume with no experience?
Use school, volunteer or part-time work where you organised, scheduled or handled records - club secretary, front-desk shifts, event coordination. Describe it with the same number-based bullets.

### Is a receptionist resume different from an administrative assistant resume?
Receptionist resumes focus on front-desk work - visitors, phones and appointments. Admin assistant resumes add scheduling, documents and finance tasks. Use the job ad's title and match its main duties.
