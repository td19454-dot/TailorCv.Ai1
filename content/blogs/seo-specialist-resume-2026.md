## Write a summary that leads with impact

A short [summary](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-summary) frames your value:

> "SEO specialist with 5 years growing organic traffic for B2B SaaS and e-commerce. Drove +140% organic sessions and a 45% lift in organic revenue through technical SEO, content strategy, and link building. Expert in Ahrefs, GA4, and Search Console."

See [resume objective vs summary](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-objective-vs-summary) for the opening lines.

## Cover the full skill set

Signal breadth in your [skills section](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-skills-section-guide-2026): keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO (crawl, indexing, Core Web Vitals, schema), content strategy, link building, and analytics. The strongest SEO specialists bridge engineering and content, so a bullet on each side signals versatility. Mirror the [job description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-analysis-checklist) and confirm coverage with the [ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions).

## Name your tools

List Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console, GA4, Screaming Frog, Surfer, and any CMS you've optimized. Tools are table stakes, so keep them tight and let your [quantified](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume) results carry the weight.

## Show technical and content range

SEO roles lean technical or content-focused, and the best specialists do both. Include a technical win (a site migration, a Core Web Vitals fix, a schema rollout) and a content win (a topic-cluster strategy, a link campaign) so you fit either flavor of role. A [portfolio](https://www.thetailorcv.com/portfolio) of case studies seals it — see [portfolio examples that landed interviews](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-examples-that-landed-interviews).

## Structure an SEO specialist resume

1. **Header** — name, title, phone, email, [portfolio](https://www.thetailorcv.com/portfolio) link.
2. **Summary** — three lines, results-forward.
3. **Skills** — technical SEO, content, analytics, tools.
4. **Experience** — company, role, [quantified](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume) traffic/revenue bullets.
5. **Education & certifications**.

This mirrors the layout in the [resume builder](https://www.thetailorcv.com/modify-cv). For ordering, see the [resume section order guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-section-order-guide).

**Technical or content SEO — which do I emphasize?**
Show both; the strongest specialists bridge them. [Tailor](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-tailor-resume-in-5-minutes) toward whichever the role leans.

**I'm breaking into SEO — what do I lead with?**
Self-directed projects — a site you grew, a blog you ranked — plus any certifications, per the [projects in resume guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/projects-in-resume).

**Do I need a portfolio?**
A [portfolio](https://www.thetailorcv.com/portfolio) of ranking case studies seals it — see [portfolio examples that landed interviews](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-examples-that-landed-interviews).
