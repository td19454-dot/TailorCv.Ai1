## What Is an ATS Resume Checker?

An [ATS](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-an-ats-score-and-why-does-it-decide-your-job-application-before-any-human-reads-it) resume checker is a tool that simulates how an Applicant Tracking System reads and ranks your resume. It analyzes your resume against a job description and produces:

- An overall [ATS score](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-score-guide)
- A list of matched and [missing keywords](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/hidden-keywords-in-job-description)
- Formatting and parseability warnings
- Concrete suggestions to improve

Think of it as a preview of what the employer's hiring software will see.

---

## How an ATS Actually Works (The 3 Stages)

### Stage 1: Parsing

The [ATS](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-an-ats-score-and-why-does-it-decide-your-job-application-before-any-human-reads-it) extracts structured data from your resume - name, contact info, work history, skills, education, and dates. If your layout is complex, parsing fails and sections get lost or jumbled.

### Stage 2: Keyword Matching

The system compares your parsed content against the [job description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-keyword-extraction-guide), looking for required skills, tools, titles, and qualifications. The closer the match, the higher you rank.

### Stage 3: Ranking

Modern [ATS](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/what-is-an-ats-score-and-why-does-it-decide-your-job-application-before-any-human-reads-it) platforms use AI and natural language processing to rank candidates by relevance, experience depth, and fit. Recruiters then review the top-ranked resumes first.

A checker mirrors all three stages so you can fix problems before applying.

## What an ATS Resume Checker Analyzes

| Area | What It Checks |
|---|---|
| Parseability | Can the software read every section cleanly? |
| Keyword match | Do you include the job's key skills and terms? |
| Formatting | Tables, columns, graphics, fonts, headings |
| Job title fit | Do your titles align with the target role? |
| Achievements | Are results measurable and specific? |
| Contact & sections | Are standard sections present and readable? |

## How to Pass an ATS Resume Checker

### Use ATS-Friendly Formatting

Single column, standard headings, no tables or graphics. Begin with an [ATS-friendly template](https://www.thetailorcv.com/templates).

### Mirror the Job Description

Use the exact skills and terminology from the posting. See the [resume keywords guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-keywords-guide).

### Include Standard Sections

Summary, Skills, Experience, Education, [certifications](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-list-certifications-on-resume) - in clear, labeled sections.

### Quantify Your Impact

Add numbers to your achievements. See [how to quantify resume achievements](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-resume-achievements).

### Submit the Right File

Use a text-based PDF or DOCX - never an image or scan.

## ATS Checker vs. Human Recruiter

A checker helps you pass the software, but your resume still needs to impress a person afterward. The good news: the same best practices - clarity, relevant keywords, [measurable results](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-resume-achievements), clean formatting - work for both. Avoid keyword stuffing, which can pass naive filters but fails human review.
