## Key Takeaways

- **PA-C after your name is the credential that matters.** NCCPA certification plus state licensure go at the top, with numbers and expiry dates.
- Specialty is a matching keyword. Emergency medicine, orthopaedics and dermatology PAs are screened against different postings.
- Procedures you can perform independently belong in their own section — this is what distinguishes PA resumes from each other.
- New graduates lead with rotations: specialty, hours, setting, and what you actually did.
- Two pages is standard and expected. Do not compress clinical detail to fit one.

Physician assistant hiring runs through credentialing before it runs through a hiring manager. NCCPA certification, state licensure, DEA registration where relevant — these are verified, and a resume that presents them clearly moves faster.

## Best Physician Assistant Resume Format for 2026

Reverse-chronological, two pages, single column.

Two pages is correct. Licensure, certification, rotations and procedures do not fit on one page without cutting content that gets verified anyway — our [resume length guide](/blog/ats-resume-length-guide) covers when the one-page rule genuinely applies.

Single column, no tables or text boxes: hospital systems often run older parsers that read multi-column layouts out of order. See [ATS formatting mistakes](/blog/ats-resume-formatting-mistakes).

Section order:

1. Header with PA-C
2. Licensure and certification
3. Professional summary
4. Clinical experience
5. Procedures
6. Education and rotations
7. Professional memberships

## Header and Credentials

```
James Okonkwo, MPAS, PA-C
Charlotte, NC · (704) 555-0188 · j.okonkwo@email.com · linkedin.com/in/jamesokonkwo
```

**PA-C** means currently certified by the NCCPA. It is the credential recruiters look for, and it is not optional shorthand — write it. Degree first (MPAS, MHS, MMS), then PA-C.

## Licensure and Certification Section

Directly under the header, with numbers and dates:

```
LICENSURE & CERTIFICATION
Physician Assistant, North Carolina, License #PA-11482, active through 08/2028
NCCPA Certified (PA-C), certified 2020, recertification due 2030
DEA Registration, active, Schedule II-V
BLS / ACLS / PALS — current through 05/2027
```

Including licence numbers and expiry dates saves credentialing a step and signals you have done this before. If you hold licences in multiple states, list them all — employers hiring for telehealth or multi-site systems specifically want this.

**DEA registration** is a hard requirement in many postings. If you have it, name it in those words.

## Specialty Is a Keyword

PA roles are specialty-specific and the first-pass matching reflects that. A posting for an orthopaedic surgery PA is not looking for generic "physician assistant" text.

Write your specialty explicitly in the summary, in your experience headers, and in the skills section where it is true:

- Emergency medicine
- Orthopaedic surgery
- Dermatology
- Cardiothoracic surgery
- Family medicine / primary care
- Hospitalist / internal medicine
- Urgent care
- Psychiatry

If you are moving between specialties, say so directly and connect your transferable clinical work rather than hiding the switch. Our [career-change resume guidance](/blog/resume-matching-for-career-changers) covers the framing.

## Professional Summary

Four lines maximum. Specialty, years certified, setting, one measurable outcome.

> Emergency medicine PA-C with 5 years in a Level II trauma centre averaging 32 patients per shift. Independently manage acute presentations across the full acuity range, with procedural competence in laceration repair, fracture reduction and central line placement. Reduced door-to-disposition time in fast-track by 22% through a PA-led triage protocol.

## Procedures Section

This is the section that differentiates PA resumes, and most candidates underuse it. List what you can perform independently:

> **Procedures**
> Laceration repair and complex wound closure · Incision and drainage · Fracture reduction and splinting · Joint injection and aspiration · Central venous line placement · Lumbar puncture · Arterial line placement · Endotracheal intubation · Chest tube insertion · Skin biopsy (punch, shave, excisional) · Nail bed repair · Foreign body removal

Volume matters where you have it — *"laceration repair (400+)"* is stronger than the bare listing.

Only list what you can discuss under questioning. This section is interview material, and a claimed procedure you cannot describe is worse than an omitted one.

## Best ATS Keywords for a PA Resume

**Clinical:** differential diagnosis, patient assessment, treatment planning, acute care, chronic disease management, diagnostic interpretation, first assist, rounding, admissions, discharge planning, consultation

**Credentials:** PA-C, NCCPA, state licensure, DEA registration, prescriptive authority, credentialing, privileging, supervisory agreement, collaborative practice

**Systems:** Epic, Cerner, Meditech, athenahealth, CPT coding, ICD-10, documentation, order entry

**Setting:** Level I/II trauma centre, outpatient clinic, ambulatory surgery, inpatient, urgent care, telehealth

## Writing Clinical Experience Bullets

**Weak:**
> Saw patients in the emergency department and provided treatment.

**Strong:**
> Independently managed 32 patients per shift across the acuity range in a Level II trauma centre, including primary evaluation, order entry, procedural intervention and disposition.

**Weak:**
> Assisted in surgery.

**Strong:**
> First-assisted on 200+ orthopaedic cases annually (total joint, arthroscopy, fracture fixation), including closure, and managed pre-operative clearance and post-operative rounding for a 4-surgeon practice.

Numbers most PAs have: patients per shift, cases first-assisted, procedures performed, panel size, RVUs, length-of-stay or throughput movement, students precepted. ## New Graduate Physician Assistants

Rotations carry the resume. Present them with the same seriousness as a job.

> **CLINICAL ROTATIONS — 2,000+ hours, 2025-2026**
> **Emergency Medicine**, Mercy General (8 weeks). Evaluated 12-15 patients per shift under supervision; performed laceration repairs, splinting, I&D; presented to attending and documented in Epic.
> **General Surgery**, St. Anne's (8 weeks). First-assisted on 40+ cases; managed pre-op workups and post-op rounds.
> **Internal Medicine**, Riverside (8 weeks). Carried 6-8 inpatients daily; admissions, daily progress notes, discharge summaries.
> **Family Medicine**, Northside Clinic (6 weeks). Independent primary evaluation of 15 patients daily.
> **Psychiatry** (4 weeks) · **Pediatrics** (4 weeks) · **Women's Health** (4 weeks)

Include prior healthcare experience — EMT, paramedic, scribe, RN, military medic. PA programmes require patient-care hours and hiring managers value what those hours taught you. Do not delete them because they predate the degree.

## Common PA Resume Mistakes

**Omitting PA-C.** It is the credential. Write it after your name.

**Burying licensure on page two.** Credentialing looks first.

**No procedures section.** It is the most differentiating part of a PA resume — see [weak resume bullets](/blog/weak-resume-bullets-fix) for the same failure in general form.

**Generic specialty language.** "Physician assistant with clinical experience" matches nothing specific.

**Cutting rotations as a new grad.** They are your clinical evidence.

**Dropping pre-PA healthcare work.** Those patient-care hours matter and interviewers ask about them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long should a PA resume be?**
Two pages. Licensure, procedures and rotations do not compress without loss.

**Do I list rotations if I have three years of experience?**
Condense them to a few lines. Once you have real clinical employment, that leads.

**Should I include DEA registration?**
Yes, if you hold it. Many postings require it explicitly.

**How do I show specialty change?**
Name the target specialty in the summary and connect the transferable procedures and patient populations directly.

**What if my licence is pending?**
Say so with the expected date: *"NC licensure application submitted, anticipated 10/2026."* Employers hire new grads on this basis routinely.
