Writing a compelling [cover letter](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-guide-2026) is a critical step in the [job application process](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-many-jobs-should-you-apply-to-per-day), especially in the U.S. job market. A well-crafted cover letter can set you apart from other candidates by showcasing your personality, skills, and [genuine interest](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-mistakes-2026) in the position. Whether you’re a new graduate, an international candidate, or someone seeking visa sponsorship, understanding how to write a cover letter tailored to U.S. employers is essential. In this article, we’ll explore the structure of a cover letter, provide practical tips, and include examples to help you create a standout cover letter that resonates with hiring managers.

## Key Takeaways

- A cover letter is essential in the U.S. job market as it showcases your personality, skills, and genuine interest in the position.
- The standard structure of a cover letter includes a header, salutation, introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion, and signature.
- Personalization is key; address the hiring manager by name and express enthusiasm for the specific role and company.
- Use the body paragraphs to highlight relevant experience and explain your alignment with the company's values.
- Always tailor each cover letter to the job you are applying for to make a strong impression.

## Understanding the Purpose of a Cover Letter

A cover letter serves several vital functions:

- **Personalization:** It allows you to address the hiring manager directly and convey your enthusiasm for the [specific role and company](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-for-internal-job-application).
- **Complementing Your Resume:** While your resume lists your qualifications and experiences, the cover letter gives context to those details and demonstrates your fit for the position.
- **Showcasing Your Writing Skills:** A well-written cover letter reflects your communication abilities, which are often critical in many roles.

## Structure of a Cover Letter

A typical cover letter for U.S. jobs follows a standard format, which includes the following sections:

A format like this parses cleanly - one column, standard headings, everything the parser needs where it expects to find it.


### 1. Header

Your cover letter should start with your contact information, followed by the date and the employer's contact information. Here’s how to format it:

```
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, Zip Code]
[Your Phone Number]
[Your Email Address]

[Date]

[Employer's Name]
[Company's Name]
[Company's Address]
[City, State, Zip Code]
```

### 2. Salutation

Address the hiring manager by name, if possible. If you cannot find a name, use a general greeting such as "Dear Hiring Manager."

### 3. Introduction

Begin with a strong opening that grabs the reader's attention. State the position you are applying for and briefly explain why you are interested in the job and the company.

**Example:**
"Dear [Hiring Manager's Name],  
I am excited to apply for the Marketing Coordinator position at [Company Name]. With a strong background in [digital marketing](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-examples-that-landed-interviews) and a passion for innovative strategies, I am eager to contribute to your team."

### 4. Body Paragraphs

This section typically contains two to three paragraphs that illustrate your qualifications:

- **First Paragraph:** Discuss your [relevant experience](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-no-work-experience) and skills. Use specific examples to demonstrate your achievements.
  
- **Second Paragraph:** Explain why you are particularly interested in the company and how your values align with theirs. This shows you’ve done your research and are genuinely interested in being part of their team.

- **Optional Third Paragraph:** If applicable, mention any [additional skills](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-for-career-change) or experiences that set you apart, such as certifications or volunteer work.

**Example:**
"In my previous role at [Previous Company], I successfully managed a digital campaign that increased web traffic by 30% within three months. I admire [Company Name] for its commitment to innovation and sustainability, and I believe my proactive approach to marketing aligns well with your mission."

### 5. Conclusion

Reiterate your enthusiasm for the position and express your desire for an interview. Thank the hiring manager for considering your application.

**Example:**
"I am enthusiastic about the opportunity to bring my expertise in [digital marketing](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/portfolio-examples-that-landed-interviews) to [Company Name]. I look forward to discussing how I can contribute to your team. Thank you for considering my application."

### 6. Signature

End with your name, and if sending a hard copy, leave space for your signature.

```
Sincerely,  
[Your Name]
```

## Tips for Writing an Effective Cover Letter

- **Tailor Each Letter:** Customize your cover letter for each job application. Highlight specific skills and experiences relevant to the job description.
- **Be Concise:** Keep your cover letter to one page. Aim for clear, succinct language that gets to the point quickly.
- **Use Action Verbs:** Start sentences with [strong action verbs](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/ats-friendly-cover-letter-format) to convey confidence (e.g., "developed," "led," "achieved").
- **Quantify Achievements:** Whenever possible, use numbers to quantify your accomplishments, making your impact clearer to employers.
- **Proofread:** Spelling and grammatical errors can undermine your professionalism. Always [proofread your cover letter](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/cover-letter-mistakes-2026) or use tools like the [AI cover letter generator](https://www.thetailorcv.com/cover-letter) for assistance.

## Navigating the U.S. Job Market as an International Candidate

If you are an international candidate or seeking visa sponsorship, it's crucial to address your [work authorization status](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-spot-a-job-scam) in your cover letter. Briefly mention your eligibility to work in the U.S. and any relevant visas you hold, as this information can be pivotal for employers considering your application.

**Example:**
"I am currently on an H-1B visa and am eligible to continue working in the U.S. I am committed to contributing my skills to [Company Name] and am excited about the potential for growth within your organization."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I tailor my cover letter for each job application?

To tailor your cover letter, carefully read the job description and highlight the skills and experiences that match the requirements. Use keywords from the job posting and demonstrate how your background aligns with the company’s values and goals. For more guidance on this process, check out our article on [resume matching with job description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-matching-with-job-description-complete-guide).

### Should I include my resume with my cover letter?

Yes, you should include your resume with your cover letter unless the job posting specifies otherwise. Your cover letter complements your resume by providing context to your experiences. For tips on creating a strong resume, refer to our [how to write a resume from scratch](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-from-scratch) guide.

### What if I have no relevant experience?

If you lack direct experience, focus on transferable skills and any relevant coursework, internships, or volunteer work. Highlight your willingness to learn and adapt. Our article on [entry-level jobs in the USA](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/entry-level-jobs-usa-new-grad) provides further strategies for showcasing your potential.

### How can I ensure my cover letter is ATS-friendly?

To make your cover letter ATS-friendly, use standard formatting, avoid images, and include relevant keywords from the job description. You can also use our [free ATS score checker](https://www.thetailorcv.com/solutions) to assess your documents' compatibility with ATS systems.

### How do I follow up after sending my cover letter?

It's appropriate to follow up one to two weeks after submitting your application. You can send a brief email expressing your continued interest and inquiring about the status of your application.
