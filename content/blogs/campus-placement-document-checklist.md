## Key Takeaways

- Placement drives frequently give a day or two of notice, so documents assembled in advance are documents you actually have.
- Marksheets for every semester, plus tenth and twelfth, are the most commonly requested set.
- Keep both digital scans and physical copies, named clearly and stored in one place.
- Discrepancies between your resume and your marksheets are checked at offer stage and cause real problems.
- A formal photograph, ID proof and a clean email address are small things that block applications when missing.

Placement season is the wrong time to discover your tenth standard marksheet is at home in another city, or that your only photograph is a cropped selfie. Drives are announced with short notice, forms have deadlines measured in hours, and a missing document is a straightforward way to lose an opportunity you had earned.

Assembling this once, before the season, takes an afternoon.

## Academic documents

**All semester marksheets.** Every semester to date, not just recent ones. Companies frequently ask for the full set at verification.

**Tenth and twelfth standard marksheets and certificates.** Many companies screen on these, and students routinely do not have them to hand.

**Degree certificate or provisional certificate**, once available.

**Consolidated marksheet or transcript**, if your institution issues one.

**Backlog clearance certificates**, if applicable — see [the campus placement backlog guide](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/campus-placement-backlog-guide).

**Bonafide or student status certificate** from your institution, which some companies request.

**CGPA to percentage conversion certificate.** Many companies specify percentage criteria while your institution reports CGPA. The conversion formula varies by university, and a certificate from your institution resolves the ambiguity. Students discover this need at the worst moment.

## Identity and personal

**Photo ID** — Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence. Have at least two forms.

**PAN card.** Required at joining for payroll, and applying for one takes time. If you do not have one, start now.

**Passport**, if you have one. Some roles involve travel and companies ask early. Applying takes weeks, so if a role might require it, do not wait.

**Formal photograph.** Plain background, formal clothing, neutral expression, recent. You will need both digital and printed copies, in passport size and stamp size.

**Bank account details**, needed at joining.

## Organising it

**One folder, two formats.**

**Digital:** a single cloud folder with clearly named PDF scans — `10th_Marksheet.pdf`, `Sem5_Marksheet.pdf`. Scans, not photographs taken at an angle. Many forms have file size limits, so keep compressed versions too.

**Physical:** a single file with photocopies of everything, plus originals kept separately and safely. Bring the file to every drive.

**Naming matters more than it sounds.** Uploading `IMG_20260812_094533.jpg` to an application portal looks careless, and some portals reject unclear filenames.

## Consistency — the thing that causes real problems

Your resume, your marksheets and your application forms are read together at verification.

**Check that these match exactly:**

- **Your name**, spelled identically everywhere. Variations between documents cause genuine delays.
- **Date of birth**, consistent across ID documents and forms.
- **CGPA or percentage**, matching your marksheets exactly rather than rounded up.
- **Dates** of internships and projects.
- **Institution name**, as officially written.

**Discrepancies get flagged at offer stage**, which is the worst possible time. A rounding difference in your CGPA is a small thing that reads as carelessness at best.

## Before each drive

A short pre-drive routine that prevents most problems:

- Read the eligibility criteria properly, including tenth and twelfth requirements
- Check which documents that specific company requires
- Confirm your resume version matches the company type
- Charge your laptop and test your internet if the round is online
- Have your file, ID and photographs with you physically
- Note the reporting time and location

## Common Mistakes

**Not having tenth and twelfth marksheets.** Many companies screen on them and students frequently leave them at home.

**No CGPA-to-percentage conversion certificate.** Companies specify percentages while institutions report CGPA, and the formula varies by university.

**Photographs of documents instead of scans.** Angled, shadowed images look careless and some portals reject them.

**Careless file names.** `IMG_20260812.jpg` on an application portal reads badly and occasionally gets rejected outright.

**Rounding your CGPA upward.** It is checked against marksheets at verification, and the discrepancy reads as dishonesty rather than approximation.

**Name spelled differently across documents.** It causes genuine delays at offer and joining stage.

**No PAN card.** It is needed at joining and applying takes time.

**Assembling documents after a drive is announced.** Notice periods are frequently a day or two, which is not enough.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which documents are asked for most often?

All semester marksheets, tenth and twelfth marksheets, photo ID, a formal photograph and your resume. Many companies also want a CGPA-to-percentage conversion certificate.

### Do I need tenth and twelfth marksheets?

Frequently yes — a meaningful number of companies screen on those marks, and students routinely do not have them available.

### What is a CGPA to percentage conversion certificate?

An institutional document stating the official conversion formula, since companies specify percentage criteria while universities report CGPA and formulas differ.

### What kind of photograph do I need?

Formal — plain background, formal clothing, neutral expression, recent. Have digital, passport size and stamp size copies.

### Should I carry physical documents to drives?

Yes, in a single file with photocopies. Some rounds verify on the spot, and having it with you costs nothing.

### What if my name is spelled differently on two documents?

Get it corrected before placement season if possible. Discrepancies cause real delays at offer and joining stage.

### When should I get a PAN card?

Now, if you do not have one. It is required at joining and the application takes time you will not have during the season.

## Make This Practical

Spend one afternoon before the season assembling everything into a single cloud folder with clear filenames, plus one physical file with photocopies. Scans rather than photographs, compressed versions for portals with size limits, and both passport and stamp size photographs.

Then check consistency across documents rather than assuming. Your name spelled identically, date of birth matching, and CGPA stated exactly as your marksheets show it — never rounded up, because verification at offer stage is precisely where that becomes a problem.

Finally, close the two gaps students discover too late: get a CGPA-to-percentage conversion certificate from your institution, and apply for a PAN card now if you do not have one. Both take time you will not have when a drive is announced with two days' notice.
