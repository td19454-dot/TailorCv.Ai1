## Accountant Resume Summary

Formula:

Accountant with X years of experience in [general ledger / AP/AR / tax / audit / financial reporting]. Proficient in [software]. Managed [account scope or transaction volume]. Improved [close time, accuracy, or compliance outcome].

### Example for Experienced Accountant

Senior Accountant with 6 years of experience in general ledger, financial reporting, and month-end close for mid-sized companies. Proficient in SAP, QuickBooks, and advanced Excel. Reduced monthly close cycle from 8 days to 5 days and maintained zero material audit findings across 4 annual audits. CPA certified with strong GAAP and IFRS knowledge.

### Example for Entry-Level Accountant

Staff Accountant with 2 years of experience in accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliation. Proficient in QuickBooks, Tally, and Excel. Processed 400+ monthly invoices with 99.8% accuracy. CA Inter qualified, seeking a staff accountant role in a corporate finance team.

## How to Write Accountant Resume Bullet Points

Formula:

Managed / Reduced / Reconciled / Prepared + [accounting process or volume] + [scope or context] + [accuracy, time, compliance, or savings outcome]

### Weak Bullet Points

- Handled accounts payable and receivable
- Prepared financial reports
- Reconciled bank statements
- Used SAP for accounting

### Strong Bullet Points

- Reduced monthly close cycle from 8 days to 5 days by automating 6 recurring journal entries and standardizing the reconciliation checklist across 4 entities.
- Processed and reconciled 600+ monthly vendor invoices with 99.7% accuracy, identifying $84K in duplicate payment recoveries over 12 months.
- Prepared monthly financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow) for a $40M revenue business unit in compliance with IFRS, supporting board-level reporting.
- Managed full-cycle accounts receivable for 200+ clients, reducing days sales outstanding (DSO) from 58 days to 41 days through a structured collections process.
- Led the company through 3 consecutive clean external audits with zero material findings by maintaining robust internal controls and audit-ready documentation.

## Common Accountant Resume Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No accuracy or efficiency metrics

Accounting is measurable. Show close cycle time, transaction volume, accuracy rate, DSO improvement, or audit results.

### Mistake 2: Listing duties, not achievements

"Prepared journal entries and reconciled accounts" is a [job description](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-keyword-extraction-guide). Show the scope and the improvement you delivered.

### Mistake 3: No software specified

Accounting roles filter heavily by software experience. List your specific ERP and accounting software - SAP, QuickBooks, Oracle, Tally, etc.

### Mistake 4: Missing compliance framework

GAAP, IFRS, SOX, or local tax compliance - name the standards you work under. This is a screening criteria.
