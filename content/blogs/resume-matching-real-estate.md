## The Three Types of Real Estate Job Descriptions

1. **Sales and leasing agent roles** — weigh production numbers, licensing, and client relationship skills heavily
2. **Property management roles** — weigh operational metrics, tenant relations, and specific software proficiency
3. **Commercial and corporate real estate roles** — weigh financial analysis, market research, and deal experience

Matching your resume starts with correctly identifying which of these three categories the posting falls into, since the language and priorities differ significantly.

## Matching a Real Estate Agent Resume

- Lead with production numbers: total sales volume, number of transactions closed, average deal size
- Confirm your license status matches exactly what the posting requires (state, brokerage affiliation)
- Reflect the specific market segment named in the posting (residential, luxury, commercial, rental)
- Name the CRM or MLS platform you use if the posting specifies one

Read [how to quantify resume achievements](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/how-to-quantify-resume-achievements) for guidance on presenting commission-based results clearly.

---

## Matching a Property Management Resume

- Name the specific property management software required (Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage) explicitly
- Quantify portfolio size: number of units, square footage, or properties managed
- Reflect occupancy rate improvements, tenant retention, or budget management results
- Match the property type language exactly (multifamily, commercial, mixed-use, student housing)

---

## Matching a Commercial Real Estate Resume

- Lead with deal volume, transaction size, or portfolio value managed or analyzed
- Reflect specific financial modeling or underwriting tools by name (Argus, Excel-based models, CoStar)
- Match the exact asset class language used in the posting (office, industrial, retail, multifamily)
- Include relevant credentials like CCIM or CPM if the posting values them

If you are moving between real estate sub-sectors, such as residential sales into commercial analysis, read [resume matching for career changers](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-matching-with-job-description-complete-guide) for guidance on translating your experience.

## Common Mistakes in Real Estate Resume Matching

### Treating All Real Estate Roles as the Same

Submitting the same resume for an agent role and a property management role dilutes your match for both. Tailor your emphasis to the specific role type.

### Omitting License or Credential Status

If a posting requires an active real estate license and your resume does not clearly confirm it, you risk being filtered out even with strong experience. State your license status explicitly.

### Leaving Out Software Proficiency

Property management and commercial real estate postings frequently name specific tools as requirements. Listing "real estate software" generically instead of naming Yardi or AppFolio directly can cost you a keyword match.

### Formatting That Fails ATS Parsing

## Real Estate Job Description Analysis: A Step-by-Step Guide

When preparing your resume for a real estate position, analyzing the job description is crucial. Here’s a step-by-step approach to ensure you capture all relevant details:



1. **Read the Job Description Thoroughly**: Begin by reading the entire job description carefully. Highlight key responsibilities, required skills, and qualifications.

2. **Identify Keywords and Phrases**: As you read, make a note of specific keywords or phrases that stand out, especially those related to skills, software, and industry terminology. These are often indicators of what the employer values most.

3. **Categorize the Information**: Divide the highlighted keywords into categories such as Skills, Experience, and Qualifications. This helps you visualize what areas you need to focus on in your resume.

4. **Cross-Reference with Your Experience**: Compare the categorized keywords with your own experiences. Identify where your background aligns with the job description and where it doesn’t.

5. **Modify Your Resume**: Tailor your resume by integrating the identified keywords and phrases into your work experience and skills sections. Ensure they reflect your actual capabilities and experiences.

6. **Review and Revise**: After making initial changes, review your resume against the job description again to ensure you haven’t missed any important elements.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Matching Your Resume

Creating a tailored resume is an art, and avoiding common pitfalls can make a significant difference. Here are some mistakes to steer clear of:

1. **Using a Generic Resume**: Submitting the same resume for multiple applications can lead to missed opportunities. Always customize your resume for each job.

2. **Ignoring Job Description Language**: Failing to use the specific terminology found in the job description can result in your resume not passing through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Always mirror the language used in the job posting.

3. **Overloading with Irrelevant Information**: Including experiences or skills that don’t relate to the job can dilute your message. Focus on what’s relevant to the position at hand.

4. **Neglecting Soft Skills**: In real estate, interpersonal skills are crucial. Don’t forget to highlight your communication, negotiation, and relationship-building abilities.

5. **Not Showcasing Results**: Employers want to see your achievements. Use quantifiable metrics (e.g., “increased sales by 20%”) to demonstrate your impact in previous roles.

By being mindful of these common mistakes, you can create a more effective and targeted resume.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do real estate companies use ATS to screen resumes?

Larger brokerages, property management firms, and commercial real estate companies increasingly do, especially for property management and corporate roles. Independent agent hiring is often more relationship-based, but [matching your resume's language](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/job-description-analysis-checklist) still helps.

### How important is naming specific software like Yardi or AppFolio?

Very important for property management roles. If a posting requires experience with a specific platform and your resume only says "property management software," you risk a [keyword mismatch](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/resume-keyword-map-template).

### Should I include my sales volume even if it varied year to year?

Yes, include your most recent or strongest year, and specify the time period. Real, specific numbers are more credible and more matchable than vague claims.

### Can I use one resume for both residential and commercial real estate roles?

Not effectively. The keywords, metrics, and priorities differ significantly between the two. Maintain separate versions and [tailor each](https://thetailorcv.com/blog/matching-portfolio-to-job-description) to the specific posting.
